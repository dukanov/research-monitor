📄 **Hierarchical Decoding for Discrete Speech Synthesis with Multi-Resolution Spoof Detection** — MSpoof-TTS introduces a training-free inference framework that uses multi-resolution spoof detectors to identify unnatural patterns in neural codec sequences at different temporal granularities, then applies hierarchical decoding to prune low-quality candidates. Unlike existing approaches that require model retraining or preference optimization, this method enhances synthesis quality purely during inference using discriminator-guided generation to address token-level artifacts and distributional drift. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.05373)

📄 **WavSLM: Single-Stream Speech Language Modeling via WavLM Distillation** — WavSLM quantizes self-supervised WavLM representations into a single codebook and trains with autoregressive next-chunk prediction, jointly modeling semantic and acoustic information in one token stream. The approach differs from existing methods by requiring no text supervision, text pretraining, or hierarchical token streams, maintaining the simplicity of single-stream generative pretraining while achieving competitive performance with fewer parameters. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.05299)

📄 **A Large-Scale Probing Analysis of Speaker-Specific Attributes in Self-Supervised Speech Representations** — This work systematically analyzes how 11 self-supervised speech models encode speaker-specific information by decomposing speaker identity into acoustic, prosodic, and paralinguistic attributes across network layers. The key finding challenges conventional wisdom by showing that larger models actually recover speaker identity in their deep layers, while intermediate layers capture dynamic prosody better than specialized speaker embeddings. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.05310)

📄 **Llama-Mimi: Exploring the Limits of Flattened Speech Language Modeling** — Llama-Mimi flattens multi-level audio tokens from the Mimi neural codec into a single sequence and models them with a standard Transformer decoder, rather than using hierarchical architectures. This approach moves away from the typical hierarchical designs used for Residual Vector Quantization tokens, adopting the NLP trend toward simpler, less architecturally biased models while outperforming hierarchical CSM-based approaches on most tasks. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.14882)
