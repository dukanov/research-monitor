📄 **Step-Audio-R1.5 Technical Report** — Step-Audio-R1.5 replaces Reinforcement Learning with Verified Rewards (RLVR) with Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF) for training audio language models with chain-of-thought reasoning. The authors identify a "verifiable reward trap" where RLVR optimizes for discrete benchmark scores but degrades prosodic quality, emotional continuity, and conversational naturalness by reducing continuous audio contexts to text-based verification tasks. Their RLHF approach preserves analytical reasoning performance while improving the naturalness of extended spoken dialogues. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.25719)
