📄 **DashengTokenizer: One layer is enough for unified audio understanding and generation** — This work introduces a continuous audio tokenizer that inverts conventional training by starting with frozen semantic features and injecting acoustic information, rather than training acoustic tokenizers first then adding semantic knowledge. The approach achieves superior performance across 22 understanding tasks while maintaining competitive reconstruction quality, challenging the assumption that VAE architectures are necessary for audio synthesis. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.23765)

📄 **Human or Machine? A Preliminary Turing Test for Speech-to-Speech Interaction** — Researchers conducted the first systematic Turing test evaluation for speech-to-speech conversational systems, collecting 2,968 human judgments across 9 state-of-the-art systems and finding none pass the human-likeness test. Unlike previous text-based evaluations, they developed a fine-grained taxonomy of 18 human-likeness dimensions and discovered that failures stem primarily from paralinguistic features and emotional expressivity rather than semantic understanding. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.24080)
