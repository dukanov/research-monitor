📄 **Scaling Open Discrete Audio Foundation Models with Interleaved Semantic, Acoustic, and Text Tokens** — SODA introduces a family of audio foundation models (135M-4B parameters) that jointly process semantic content, acoustic details, and text through next-token prediction on discrete audio tokens. Unlike existing text-first or semantic-only approaches, SODA natively handles both semantic and acoustic information in a unified architecture. The work includes the first scaling law study for discrete audio models, finding optimal data grows 1.6× faster than model size. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.16687)

📄 **Voice Impression Control in Zero-Shot TTS** — This work enables control of perceptual voice characteristics (e.g., dark-bright, rough-smooth) in zero-shot text-to-speech using low-dimensional impression vectors. The approach extends beyond speaker fidelity control to manipulate voice impressions, with the ability to generate target characteristics from natural language descriptions via large language models. This represents a novel capability for zero-shot TTS systems to control perceptual voice qualities rather than just speaker identity. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.05688)
