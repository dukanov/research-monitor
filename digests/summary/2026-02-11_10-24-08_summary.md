📄 **Covo-Audio Technical Report** — Presents a 7B-parameter Large Audio Language Model that processes continuous audio inputs and generates audio outputs within a unified architecture, eliminating the need for separate speech recognition and synthesis components. The approach introduces an "intelligence-speaker decoupling strategy" that separates dialogue reasoning from voice synthesis, enabling flexible voice customization while maintaining end-to-end audio processing capabilities. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.09823)

🤖 **MOSS-VoiceGenerator** — A 1.7B parameter text-to-speech model that generates custom speaker voices directly from free-form text descriptions, bypassing the need for reference audio samples typical in voice cloning systems. The unified MossTTSDelay architecture jointly processes voice description prompts and target text to synthesize speech with specified acoustic characteristics, enabling zero-shot voice design from textual specifications alone. [Link](https://huggingface.co/OpenMOSS-Team/MOSS-VoiceGenerator)

🤖 **MOSS-TTS-Realtime** — A streaming text-to-speech model that conditions speech synthesis on multi-turn dialogue context rather than processing each utterance independently, maintaining voice consistency and natural prosody across extended conversations. The model leverages up to 32K tokens of dialogue history and is trained on 2.5M hours of single-speaker plus 1M hours of conversational data, supporting real-time generation for interactive applications. [Link](https://huggingface.co/OpenMOSS-Team/MOSS-TTS-Realtime)
