📄 **JAM-Flow: Joint Audio-Motion Synthesis with Flow Matching** — This work introduces a unified framework that simultaneously generates facial motion and speech using flow matching with a Multi-Modal Diffusion Transformer architecture. Unlike existing approaches that treat talking head synthesis and text-to-speech as separate tasks, JAM-Flow addresses both modalities within a single model through Motion-DiT and Audio-DiT modules connected by selective joint attention layers. The method supports diverse conditioning inputs including text, reference audio, and motion through an inpainting-style training objective. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.23552)
