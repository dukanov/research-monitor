📄 **MiniCPM-o 4.5: Towards Real-Time Full-Duplex Omni-Modal Interaction** — This work presents a 9B parameter multimodal model that performs simultaneous perception and generation across vision, audio, and speech modalities using an "Omni-Flow" framework that temporally aligns all input and output streams. Unlike conventional models that operate in alternating perception-response cycles, MiniCPM-o 4.5 enables continuous, proactive interaction behaviors while maintaining real-time performance on resource-constrained edge devices. The approach represents a shift from reactive speech synthesis systems toward integrated omni-modal architectures that can generate speech as part of continuous multimodal understanding.
