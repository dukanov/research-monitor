📄 **Sign-to-Speech Prosody Transfer via Sign Reconstruction-based GAN** — Proposes directly transferring emotional and prosodic information from sign language to synthesized speech, bypassing the traditional text intermediate representation that strips away expressive elements. The SignRecGAN framework learns cross-modal prosody transfer using only unimodal datasets through adversarial learning, avoiding the need for expensive parallel sign-speech annotations that existing approaches require. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.10413)

📄 **MimicLM: Zero-Shot Voice Imitation through Autoregressive Modeling of Pseudo-Parallel Speech Corpora** — Trains voice imitation models using pseudo-parallel data where synthetic speech serves as the source and real recordings as targets, enabling learning from real speech distributions rather than being constrained by synthetic speech quality ceilings. Unlike existing methods that use complex disentanglement architectures or are limited by synthetic training targets, this approach incorporates interleaved text-audio modeling with preference alignment to handle distributional mismatch. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.11552)

📄 **Knowing What to Stress: A Discourse-Conditioned Text-to-Speech Benchmark** — Introduces CAST, a benchmark testing whether TTS systems can infer contextually appropriate word-level stress from discourse context using contrastive sentence pairs requiring different emphasis patterns. The work reveals that while text-only language models successfully identify stress targets, current TTS systems fail to realize this stress in actual speech output, differing from existing evaluations that focus on general expressiveness rather than specific discourse-conditioned stress placement. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.10580)

📄 **HumDial-EIBench: A Human-Recorded Multi-Turn Emotional Intelligence Benchmark for Audio Language Models** — Evaluates emotional intelligence in audio language models using authentic human-recorded multi-turn dialogues reformulated as multiple-choice questions to reduce scoring bias. Unlike existing benchmarks that rely on synthesized speech and single-turn interactions, this approach includes novel acoustic-semantic conflict tasks to test robustness against contradictory multimodal signals, revealing current models' struggles with multi-turn reasoning and text-dominance bias. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.11594)

📄 **CoMelSinger: Discrete Token-Based Zero-Shot Singing Synthesis With Structured Melody Control and Guidance** — Presents a zero-shot singing synthesis system using discrete tokens and structured melody control built on non-autoregressive MaskGCT architecture with lyric and pitch tokens. The approach addresses "prosody leakage" where pitch information becomes entangled with timbre prompts through coarse-to-fine contrastive learning that explicitly separates melody control from acoustic prompts, differing from conventional text-input approaches. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.19883)

📄 **Bridging What the Model Thinks and How It Speaks: Self-Aware Speech Language Models for Expressive Speech Generation** — Addresses the gap between semantic understanding and acoustic realization in Speech Language Models through Intent-Aware Bridging (converting semantics to expressive intent) and Realization-Aware Alignment (using the model as its own critic). Unlike existing approaches, SA-SLM explicitly verifies that acoustic output matches intended expression, achieving performance close to GPT-4o-Audio on expressiveness benchmarks with only 800 hours of training data. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.11424)
