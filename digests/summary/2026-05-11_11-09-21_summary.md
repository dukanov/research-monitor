📄 **S2S-Arena: Evaluating Paralinguistic Instruction Following in Speech-to-Speech Models** — Introduces a speech-native benchmark for evaluating speech-to-speech models on instruction following tasks while preserving paralinguistic features like prosody and emotion. Unlike existing text-based evaluation methods, S2S-Arena conducts arena-style pairwise comparisons directly in the speech domain across four complexity levels. Evaluation of 10 systems revealed significant performance gaps between academic and industrial models, particularly on complex paralinguistic tasks. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.05085)

📄 **VITA-QinYu: Expressive Spoken Language Model for Role-Playing and Singing** — Presents the first end-to-end spoken language model that extends beyond natural conversation to support expressive tasks including role-playing and singing. Uses a hybrid speech-text paradigm with multi-codebook audio tokens to capture richer paralinguistic information while maintaining separate modalities for different task types. Demonstrates improved performance on expressive benchmarks while maintaining competitive results on standard conversational metrics. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06765)
