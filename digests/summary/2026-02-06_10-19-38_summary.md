📄 **TASTE: Text-Aligned Speech Tokenization and Embedding for Spoken Language Modeling** — Introduces an end-to-end method for automatically learning speech tokens aligned with text transcriptions using attention-based aggregation and reconstruction objectives. Unlike previous approaches that require manual alignment procedures, TASTE learns text-aligned representations automatically while reducing token sequence length and preserving paralinguistic information. The method enables joint speech-text modeling and shows competitive performance on speech continuation tasks. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.07053)

📄 **Reasoning Beyond Majority Vote: An Explainable SpeechLM Framework for Speech Emotion Recognition** — Presents a Speech Language Model framework that generates both emotion predictions and natural language explanations for the reasoning behind each prediction using lexical and acoustic cues. Rather than relying solely on majority-voted classification labels, this approach frames emotion recognition as a generative reasoning task with rationale supervision from teacher LLMs. The method maintains competitive performance while producing human-judged plausible explanations for emotion predictions. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.24187)

📄 **UniAudio 2.0: A Unified Audio Language Model with Text-Aligned Factorized Audio Tokenization** — Introduces ReasoningCodec, which factorizes audio into reasoning tokens (text-aligned high-level representations) and reconstruction tokens (acoustic cues for waveform reconstruction) within a unified autoregressive model. This factorized design differs from existing approaches by enabling both audio understanding and generation tasks with the same tokenization scheme. The model achieves understanding performance comparable to continuous representations while improving generation quality over prior discrete tokenizers. [Link](https://huggingface.co/papers/2602.04683)

📄 **Beyond Fixed Frames: Dynamic Character-Aligned Speech Tokenization** — Proposes DyCAST, a neural audio codec that uses variable-frame-rate tokenization by aligning speech tokens with character-level linguistic units rather than uniform time intervals. Unlike conventional fixed-frame-rate codecs, this approach incorporates soft character-level alignment during training and explicit duration modeling for direct control over token durations. The system includes retrieval-augmented decoding to maintain speech quality at low frame rates while using significantly fewer tokens than existing methods. [Link](https://huggingface.co/papers/2601.23174)
