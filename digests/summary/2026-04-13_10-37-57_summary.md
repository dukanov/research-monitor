📄 **Enhancing Conversational TTS with Cascaded Prompting and ICL-Based Online Reinforcement Learning** — This work presents a cascaded TTS framework that combines textual style tokens with audio prompts to enable single-shot adaptation to fine-grained speaking styles, framing audio prompting as an in-context learning problem. The key contribution is an online reinforcement learning strategy that optimizes prosody models using subjective aesthetic rewards while preserving intelligibility through CTC alignment constraints, addressing the data bottleneck of requiring large-scale annotated datasets for expressive speech control.
