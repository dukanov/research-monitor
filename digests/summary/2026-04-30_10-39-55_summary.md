📄 **EmoTransCap: Dataset and Pipeline for Emotion Transition-Aware Speech Captioning in Discourses** — This work introduces the first large-scale dataset and automated pipeline for modeling dynamic emotion transitions across entire conversations, rather than static emotions in isolated utterances. The approach uses a Multi-Task Emotion Transition Recognition model that jointly performs emotion transition detection and speaker diarization. Unlike existing single-emotion speech captioning systems, this enables modeling of temporal emotional dynamics at the discourse level for more realistic conversational speech synthesis. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.26417)

📄 **The False Resonance: A Critical Examination of Emotion Embedding Similarity for Speech Generation Evaluation** — This paper demonstrates that cosine similarity between emotion embeddings (like emotion2vec) is unreliable for evaluating emotional speech generation quality, despite widespread adoption in the field. Through adversarial testing, the authors show that these metrics are corrupted by linguistic and speaker variations, leading them to reward acoustic mimicry rather than genuine emotional expression. The findings reveal a significant misalignment between these similarity-based metrics and human perception of emotional expressiveness in synthesized speech. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.26347)
