📄 **HoliTok: A Continuous Holistic Tokenization with Robust Dual Capabilities of Speech Generation and Understanding** — HoliTok introduces a continuous speech tokenization method that encodes 48 kHz speech into 25 Hz sequences of 128-dimensional latents, targeting unified speech generation and understanding tasks. Unlike existing discrete tokenizers, it employs progressive training to simultaneously preserve signal fidelity, semantic content, and language model compatibility within a single autoregressive + diffusion transformer architecture. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29948)

📄 **MELD: Mel-Spectrogram-Based Speech Language Modeling with Discrete Latent Variables** — MELD jointly optimizes both the encoder and speech language model on mel spectrograms, contrasting with existing methods that train these components separately. This joint optimization approach demonstrates improved performance on zero-shot text-to-speech and speech-to-text tasks while reducing common autoregressive issues like prolonged silences and word omissions. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29859)

📄 **Native Audio-Visual Alignment for Generation** — NAVA presents a two-stage approach for joint audio-video generation that first establishes correspondence in a dedicated interaction space, then applies context conditioning during joint denoising via an Align-then-Fuse MMDiT architecture. This method differs from dual-tower designs with limited audio-video co-evolution and fully unified tri-modal approaches that conflate semantic conditioning with low-level synchronization. The approach achieves competitive results with 6.3B parameters and includes controllable speech timbre through Timbre-in-Context Conditioning. [Link](https://huggingface.co/papers/2605.30073)
