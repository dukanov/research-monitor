📄 **In-Context Learning in Speech Language Models: Analyzing the Role of Acoustic Features, Linguistic Structure, and Induction Heads** — This work extends in-context learning research from text-only models to the speech domain, analyzing how acoustic features affect both content accuracy and acoustic mimicry in text-to-speech tasks. The authors find that speaking rate strongly influences ICL performance and gets reproduced in outputs, while pitch and intensity have minimal impact. They demonstrate that induction heads play a causal role in speech-based ICL, with ablation of top-k induction heads completely eliminating ICL capabilities. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.06356)

📄 **Mismatch Aware Guidance for Robust Emotion Control in Auto-Regressive TTS Models** — This paper addresses style-content mismatch in auto-regressive TTS models by proposing an adaptive Classifier-Free Guidance scheme that dynamically adjusts guidance strength based on detected mismatch levels. Unlike existing fixed CFG approaches, this method uses large language models or natural language inference to detect conflicts between emotional prompts and text semantics. The adaptive approach improves emotional expressiveness while preserving audio quality in auto-regressive TTS, where CFG application has been previously underexplored. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.13293)
