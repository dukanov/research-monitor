📄 **MSR-Codec: A Low-Bitrate Multi-Stream Residual Codec for High-Fidelity Speech Generation with Information Disentanglement** — MSR-Codec introduces a novel multi-stream architecture that encodes speech into four separate streams representing semantic content, timbre, prosody, and residual information, enabling independent manipulation of these speech components. Unlike conventional codecs that encode speech as a single representation, this disentangled approach allows for targeted modification of specific speech attributes while maintaining high-fidelity reconstruction at competitive low bitrates. When integrated into a two-stage language model for TTS, the codec achieves state-of-the-art word error rates and demonstrates effectiveness for voice conversion tasks where speaker characteristics and prosody can be controlled independently.
