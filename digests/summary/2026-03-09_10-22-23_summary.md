📄 **Prosodic Boundary-Aware Streaming Generation for LLM-Based TTS with Streaming Text Input** — This work enables streaming text-to-speech synthesis with incrementally received text by training LLM-based TTS models to stop generation at prosodic boundaries when given limited future context. The approach differs from existing methods by explicitly addressing unnatural prosody from missing lookahead text and performance collapse from unbounded context accumulation through prosodic-boundary-aware post-training and sliding-window context management. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.06444)

📄 **Efficient Emotion and Speaker Adaptation in LLM-Based TTS via Characteristic-Specific Partial Fine-Tuning** — CSP-FT selectively fine-tunes only the two layers that capture the most and least emotion/speaker information in LLM-based TTS models, updating just ~8% of parameters while maintaining performance comparable to full fine-tuning. This approach differs from existing uniform parameter fine-tuning by using dynamic layer contribution analysis to identify and target specific layers, resulting in 2x faster training and reduced catastrophic forgetting. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.14273)

📄 **Reconstruct! Don't Encode: Self-Supervised Representation Reconstruction Loss for High-Intelligibility and Low-Latency Streaming Neural Audio Codec** — The paper introduces self-supervised representation reconstruction (SSRR) loss, where neural audio codecs learn to reconstruct distilled self-supervised speech representations from their outputs rather than optimizing mel-spectrogram reconstruction. Unlike previous semantic encoder distillation approaches, SSRR directly ensures intelligibility preservation in reconstructed speech while enabling zero-lookahead streaming architectures for real-time deployment. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.05887)

📄 **Activation Steering for Accent-Neutralized Zero-Shot Text-To-Speech** — This work presents a training-free method for neutralizing accents in zero-shot TTS while preserving speaker timbre, using activation steering vectors derived from internal model activation differences between accented and native speech. The approach differs from existing methods by requiring no model retraining—steering vectors are extracted offline and applied during inference, addressing the challenge of disentangling accent from voice characteristics that existing approaches struggle with. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.05977)

📄 **StreamVoiceAnon+: Emotion-Preserving Streaming Speaker Anonymization via Frame-Level Acoustic Distillation** — StreamVoiceAnon+ addresses the common problem of emotion degradation during speaker anonymization in neural audio codec language models through supervised finetuning with neutral-emotion pairs and frame-level emotion distillation. The method achieves 24% relative improvement in emotion preservation while maintaining privacy protection, differing from existing approaches by explicitly targeting the emotion preservation challenge that neural codec models typically face during anonymization. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.06079)

📄 **ParaS2S: Benchmarking and Aligning Spoken Language Models for Paralinguistic-aware Speech-to-Speech Interaction** — ParaS2S introduces a reinforcement learning framework for training speech-to-speech models to handle paralinguistic cues and respond appropriately in both content and speaking style at the waveform level. The work differs from existing supervised approaches by using RL with an automatic judge and "PolyTone training" to prevent style hallucination, revealing that current S2S models perform poorly on paralinguistic tasks and often no better than pipeline baselines. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.08723)
