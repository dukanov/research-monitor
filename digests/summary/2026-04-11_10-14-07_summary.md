🤖 **gluschenko/omnivoice-onnx** — OmniVoice presents a zero-shot text-to-speech model supporting over 600 languages using a diffusion language model architecture that enables both voice cloning from short reference audio and voice design through controllable speaker attributes. The model achieves fast inference speeds (RTF as low as 0.025) while claiming broader language coverage than existing zero-shot TTS approaches. This ONNX-optimized version targets deployment efficiency for the original architecture. [Link](https://huggingface.co/gluschenko/omnivoice-onnx)
