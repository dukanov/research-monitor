📄 **Voice "Cloning" is Style Transfer** — This study reveals that current voice cloning systems don't faithfully reproduce speaker characteristics but instead systematically apply style transfer, making cloned voices sound more authoritative and trustworthy than originals. The work demonstrates that cloning homogenizes speaker traits like accent and speaking rate, identifying significant behavioral risks rather than proposing technical solutions. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16578)

📄 **Omni-Customizer: End-to-End MultiModal Customization for Joint Audio-Video Generation** — This framework enables simultaneous preservation of visual identities and vocal timbres across multiple subjects in joint audio-video generation. The key innovation is an Omni-Context Fusion module that enriches text prompts with multimodal identity cues, combined with Masked TTS Cross-Attention to prevent speech leakage between subjects. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.17488)

📄 **TED-TTS: Training-Free Intra-Utterance Emotion and Duration Control for Text-to-Speech Synthesis** — This approach provides fine-grained emotion and duration control within individual utterances, contrasting with existing methods that only offer utterance-level control. The framework uses segment-aware conditioning with causal masking and works with pretrained zero-shot TTS models without requiring additional training. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.03170)

📄 **SeamlessEdit: Background Noise Aware Zero-Shot Speech Editing with in-Context Enhancement** — This zero-shot speech editing framework operates in noisy real-world environments, unlike existing methods that require clean speech input. The approach uses frequency-band-aware noise suppression and in-context refinement to handle scenarios where environmental noise overlaps with speech frequency bands. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.14066)

📄 **SemaVoice: Semantic-Aware Continuous Autoregressive Speech Synthesis** — This method addresses semantic coherence issues in continuous autoregressive synthesis where models over-focus on low-level acoustic details, leading to error accumulation. The approach introduces Speech Foundation Model guided alignment to refine continuous speech representations for better semantic consistency, combined with patch-wise diffusion for synthesis. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16964)
