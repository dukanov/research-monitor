📄 **Causal Prosody Mediation for Text-to-Speech: Counterfactual Training of Duration, Pitch, and Energy in FastSpeech2** — This work introduces a causal framework that augments FastSpeech2 with counterfactual training objectives to disentangle emotional prosody from linguistic content. The approach uses two causal loss terms that enforce emotion to affect speech only through prosody variables (duration, pitch, energy) while encouraging distinct prosody patterns for different emotions, differing from existing methods that don't explicitly model causal relationships between emotion and prosody. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.11683)

📄 **Resurfacing Paralinguistic Awareness in Large Audio Language Models** — This paper addresses how Large Audio Language Models typically ignore paralinguistic cues like tone and emotion, responding only to speech content. The authors propose a paralinguistic-enhanced fine-tuning protocol that uses layer-wise analysis to identify which model layers process paralinguistic versus semantic information, then selectively fine-tunes only the paralinguistic layers with an auxiliary classification head. This targeted approach achieves better paralinguistic awareness than full model fine-tuning while being more computationally efficient. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.11947)

📄 **ID-LoRA: Identity-Driven Audio-Video Personalization with In-Context LoRA** — ID-LoRA jointly generates personalized video and audio within a single diffusion model, using text prompts, reference images, and audio clips to control both visual appearance and voice simultaneously. Unlike existing methods that treat video and audio generation as separate tasks, this approach enables synchronized audio-visual generation where the audio model has access to visual context. The method introduces technical innovations including negative temporal positioning and identity guidance to preserve speaker characteristics during denoising. [Link](https://huggingface.co/papers/2603.10256)
