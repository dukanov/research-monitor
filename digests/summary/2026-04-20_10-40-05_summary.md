📄 **Hierarchical Codec Diffusion for Video-to-Speech Generation** — HiCoDiT generates speech from silent video by explicitly modeling speech hierarchy using discrete tokens from Residual Vector Quantization codecs, where different token levels capture distinct speech attributes from speaker identity to fine-grained prosody. Unlike existing video-to-speech methods that treat speech as a flat representation, this approach uses separate processing blocks for different hierarchical levels and applies dual-scale normalization for improved coarse-to-fine modeling. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.15923)

📄 **NVBench: A Benchmark for Speech Synthesis with Non-Verbal Vocalizations** — This work introduces the first standardized benchmark for evaluating text-to-speech systems' ability to generate non-verbal vocalizations like laughs, sighs, and sobs, featuring a 45-type taxonomy and multi-axis evaluation protocol. The benchmark addresses the lack of unified evaluation frameworks by separating speech quality assessment from NVV-specific metrics like controllability and placement, revealing that NVV generation capability often decouples from overall speech quality. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.16211)

📄 **Qwen3.5-Omni Technical Report** — This multimodal model processes text, vision, and audio through a Hybrid Attention Mixture-of-Experts architecture trained on over 100 million hours of audio-visual content. The key speech synthesis contribution is ARIA, a dynamic alignment system that improves streaming speech synthesis stability by better coordinating text and speech tokenizers, addressing tokenizer mismatch issues in existing approaches. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.15804)
