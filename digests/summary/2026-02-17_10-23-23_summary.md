📄 **GSRM: Generative Speech Reward Model for Speech RLHF** — Introduces a reasoning-based speech naturalness evaluator that uses chain-of-thought reasoning after acoustic feature extraction, rather than directly regressing audio to quality scores. Unlike existing naturalness evaluators with limited interpretability, GSRM provides explainable judgments and demonstrates better cross-domain generalization, achieving human-level correlation on expert ratings. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.13891)

📄 **Investigation for Relative Voice Impression Estimation** — Proposes a framework for predicting perceptual differences between two utterances from the same speaker along semantic dimensions (e.g., "Dark-Bright") using relative comparisons rather than absolute impression scores. Self-supervised speech representations significantly outperform classical acoustic features for capturing complex voice impressions, while multimodal large language models prove unreliable for fine-grained pairwise comparisons. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.14172)

📄 **Disentangling Pitch and Creak for Speaker Identity Preservation in Speech Synthesis** — Uses conditional continuous normalizing flows to separate vocal creak from pitch during training data augmentation, enabling independent manipulation of creak while preserving speaker identity. The approach recognizes that despite statistical correlation between creak and low pitch across speakers, these features can be controlled independently without compromising speaker verification performance. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.14686)
