📄 **FlexiCodec: A Dynamic Neural Audio Codec for Low Frame Rates** — FlexiCodec introduces a neural audio codec that dynamically adjusts frame rates from 3-12.5Hz by merging semantically similar frames in information-sparse regions, using ASR features and dual-stream Transformer encoding. Unlike existing fixed-rate codecs operating at 12.5Hz, FlexiCodec allows inference-time frame rate control and better preserves semantic information at very low rates through its adaptive merging approach. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.00981)

📄 **The Design Space of Tri-Modal Masked Diffusion Models** — This work presents the first tri-modal masked diffusion model trained from scratch on text, image, and audio data, systematically analyzing scaling laws across modalities with a unified 3B-parameter architecture. Unlike previous approaches that adapt unimodal models for bimodal tasks, this method trains a single model on 6.4T tokens demonstrating capabilities across text generation, text-to-image, and text-to-speech synthesis. The authors position this as preliminary work exploring large-scale multimodal discrete diffusion rather than a fully optimized system. [Link](https://huggingface.co/papers/2602.21472)
