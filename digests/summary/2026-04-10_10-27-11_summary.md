📄 **CapTalk: Unified Voice Design for Single-Utterance and Dialogue Speech Generation** — This work extends text-to-speech synthesis from single utterances to multi-turn dialogue contexts using natural language voice descriptions. Unlike existing approaches that focus on isolated utterances, CapTalk employs speaker-level captions for dialogue modeling and introduces a Chain-of-Thought control sequence for turn-level expression planning, combined with hierarchical variational conditioning to maintain speaker consistency while adapting expression to context.
[Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08363)

📄 **Lexical Tone is Hard to Quantize: Probing Discrete Speech Units in Mandarin and Yorùbá** — This study reveals that standard quantization methods for discrete speech units from self-supervised learning models fail to preserve lexical tone information in tonal languages, despite the original representations encoding tone effectively. The authors demonstrate this limitation across multiple quantization approaches and propose a two-stage clustering method that first captures phonetic structure, then applies clustering to residual representations to better retain suprasegmental features like tone.
[Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07467)
