📄 **CoSyncDiT: Cognitive Synchronous Diffusion Transformer for Movie Dubbing** — Proposes a flow matching-based diffusion transformer that synthesizes speech matching a reference voice while synchronizing with target video lip movements through a three-stage cognitive process-inspired architecture. Unlike existing methods that use implicit alignment and suffer from interference issues, this approach employs Joint Semantic and Alignment Regularization to explicitly handle acoustic style adaptation, visual calibration, and context alignment separately. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.12292)

📄 **Beyond Transcription: Unified Audio Schema for Perception-Aware AudioLLMs** — Introduces Unified Audio Schema (UAS) that structures audio supervision into three explicit components—Transcription, Paralinguistics, and Non-linguistic Events—within a unified JSON format for training AudioLLMs. This approach differs from traditional ASR-centric training that treats non-linguistic audio as noise, improving fine-grained acoustic perception by 10.9% while preserving reasoning capabilities. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.12506)

📄 **ZipVoice-Dialog: Non-Autoregressive Spoken Dialogue Generation with Flow Matching** — Presents a non-autoregressive flow-matching model for generating spoken dialogues with realistic turn-taking and distinct speaker voices, using curriculum learning for robust speech-text alignment and speaker-turn embeddings for turn-taking control. The approach addresses the high latency and stability issues of existing autoregressive dialogue generation methods while introducing OpenDialog, a 6.8k-hour open-source dialogue dataset. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.09318)

🤖 **Darwin-TTS-1.7B-Cross** — Demonstrates cross-modal capability transfer by blending 3% of feed-forward network weights from a large language model into a text-to-speech model without training, reportedly adding emotional expressiveness to speech synthesis. The approach requires identical transformer architectures between source and target models and uses direct weight interpolation across FFN tensors, differing from training-based transfer methods but showing brittleness with narrow tolerance windows. [Link](https://huggingface.co/FINAL-Bench/Darwin-TTS-1.7B-Cross)
