📄 **Borderless Long Speech Synthesis** — This work presents a unified framework for long-form speech synthesis that maintains global context awareness across complex scenarios involving multiple speakers, interruptions, and changing emotions through hierarchical "Global-Sentence-Token" annotations and Chain-of-Thought reasoning. Unlike traditional TTS systems that process sentences independently, the approach creates a structured interface between LLM agents and the synthesis engine, enabling control from high-level semantics to phonetic details. The system extends beyond text-to-speech by accepting multimodal inputs through a front-end LLM that converts them into structured generation commands. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.19798)

📄 **DiFlowDubber: Discrete Flow Matching for Automated Video Dubbing via Cross-Modal Alignment and Synchronization** — This paper introduces the first video dubbing framework based on discrete flow matching, employing a two-stage training process where a zero-shot TTS system is pre-trained and then adapted for dubbing through cross-modal alignment. The key innovation combines a Synchronizer for lip synchronization with a Face-to-Prosody Mapper that extracts prosodic cues from facial expressions, enabling simultaneous optimization of content accuracy, prosody, acoustics, and lip sync. This differs from existing approaches by jointly addressing multiple dubbing requirements rather than handling them as separate post-processing steps. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.14267)
