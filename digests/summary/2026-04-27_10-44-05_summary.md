📄 **UniSonate: A Unified Model for Speech, Music, and Sound Effect Generation with Text Instructions** — This work presents a single flow-matching framework that generates speech, music, and sound effects from natural language instructions using a Multimodal Diffusion Transformer with dynamic token injection. Unlike existing approaches that train separate models for text-to-speech, text-to-music, and text-to-audio tasks, UniSonate unifies these under a standardized instruction interface and demonstrates that joint training across modalities improves performance through positive transfer learning. The authors report state-of-the-art results on instruction-based TTS while maintaining competitive performance on music and sound effect generation. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.22209)
