📄 **ATRIE: Adaptive Tuning for Robust Inference and Emotion in Persona-Driven Speech Synthesis** — Introduces a dual-track architecture that separates voice generation into a static Timbre Track for identity preservation and a dynamic Prosody Track for emotional expression, with knowledge distilled from a 14B language model. This disentanglement approach addresses the challenge of maintaining consistent character identity while expressing diverse emotions, achieving strong performance in speaker verification (EER: 0.04) on anime character voices. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.19055)

📄 **Affectron: Emotional Speech Synthesis with Affective and Contextually Aligned Nonverbal Vocalizations** — Tackles the generation of contextually appropriate nonverbal vocalizations (laughter, sighs) in emotional speech through an NV-augmented training strategy and structural masking applied to pre-trained verbal speech models. Unlike existing approaches, it explicitly addresses limited supervision for nonverbal vocalizations through data augmentation while integrating NV generation into established speech synthesis backbones. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.14432)

📄 **Computational Narrative Understanding for Expressive Text-to-Speech** — Introduces LibriQuote, a 5.3K hour dataset of expressive speech from audiobook character dialogue with contextual pseudo-labels describing delivery style. Rather than using general speech corpora, the approach leverages the natural expressiveness found in fictional character quotations, demonstrating improved expressivity and intelligibility when fine-tuning flow-matching and autoregressive TTS models. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.04072)
