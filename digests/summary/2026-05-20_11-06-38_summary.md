📄 **Cross-modal Consistency Guidance for Robust Emotion Control in Auto-Regressive TTS Models** — This work addresses the challenge of generating speech with target emotions that conflict with textual semantics in auto-regressive TTS models. The authors propose CCG-CFG, which uses dynamic scaling based on text-speech emotion inconsistency and replaces traditional dropout conditions with text emotion guidance, combined with hard-sample mining for distillation. When applied to CosyVoice2, the method achieves substantial improvements in both emotion recognition accuracy (12% absolute) and subjective quality scores compared to existing approaches.
