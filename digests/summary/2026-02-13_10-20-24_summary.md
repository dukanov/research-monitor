📄 **WavBench: Benchmarking Reasoning, Colloquialism, and Paralinguistics for End-to-End Spoken Dialogue Models** — WavBench introduces a three-part evaluation framework specifically designed for spoken dialogue models, assessing reasoning capabilities, natural colloquial speech patterns, and paralinguistic understanding. Unlike existing benchmarks that apply text-generation evaluation standards to speech, this benchmark focuses on audio-centric characteristics like natural vocabulary, linguistic fluency, and acoustic features essential for conversational AI. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.12135)

💻 **OpenMOSS/MOSS-Audio-Tokenizer** — MOSS-Audio-Tokenizer implements a Causal Transformer-based audio tokenizer trained on 3 million hours of diverse audio data to create discrete representations for speech, music, and other audio types. The system supports streaming processing with variable bitrates and serves as a unified interface for both audio generation and understanding tasks. [Link](https://github.com/OpenMOSS/MOSS-Audio-Tokenizer)

📄 **MOSS-Audio-Tokenizer: Scaling Audio Tokenizers for Future Audio Foundation Models** — This work presents a 1.6B parameter discrete audio tokenizer using a purely Transformer-based architecture (CAT) that jointly optimizes encoder, quantizer, and decoder components end-to-end on 3M hours of audio data. Unlike existing approaches that rely on pretrained encoders, semantic distillation, or CNN architectures, this homogeneous Transformer design achieves superior reconstruction fidelity across speech, sound, and music domains while scaling gracefully. [Link](https://huggingface.co/papers/2602.10934)
