📄 **MOSS-Audio-Tokenizer: Scaling Audio Tokenizers for Future Audio Foundation Models** — This work presents a 1.6B parameter audio tokenizer using a purely Transformer-based architecture (CAT) trained end-to-end on 3 million hours of audio data. Unlike existing approaches that rely on pretrained encoders, semantic distillation, or CNN architectures, this homogeneous Transformer design learns tokenization from scratch and demonstrates consistent scaling improvements across speech, sound, and music domains. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.10934)

📄 **RE-LLM: Refining Empathetic Speech-LLM Responses by Integrating Emotion Nuance** — This speech-based LLM incorporates dimensional emotion embeddings to generate more empathetic responses by processing speech input directly rather than relying on text transcriptions. The key innovation focuses on "emotional exploration" (deeper engagement) beyond traditional "emotional reflection," leveraging speech's richer emotional nuances that text cannot capture, achieving 35-139% relative improvement in exploration metrics. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.10716)

📄 **MaskVCT: Masked Voice Codec Transformer for Zero-Shot Voice Conversion With Increased Controllability via Multiple Guidances** — This zero-shot voice conversion model uses multiple classifier-free guidances to provide independent control over speaker identity, linguistic content, and prosodic factors within a single framework. Unlike previous approaches with fixed conditioning schemes, it allows users to selectively apply different linguistic features and optionally include pitch contour to balance intelligibility, speaker similarity, and prosody control. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.17143)

🤖 **MOSS-TTS-Local-Transformer** — MOSS-TTS is a family of open-source production-ready TTS models supporting zero-shot voice cloning, ultra-long synthesis (up to 1 hour), and fine-grained token/phoneme-level control. The approach differs from existing work by offering specialized models for different tasks (dialogue, voice generation, sound effects, real-time synthesis) that can work independently or together, with strong multilingual and code-switching capabilities. [Link](https://huggingface.co/OpenMOSS-Team/MOSS-TTS-Local-Transformer)
