📄 **SpeechParaling-Bench: A Comprehensive Benchmark for Paralinguistic-Aware Speech Generation** — Introduces a comprehensive benchmark evaluating paralinguistic control in Large Audio-Language Models, expanding from <50 to 100+ fine-grained features across three challenging tasks. The approach uses pairwise comparison with LALM-based judges rather than subjective human evaluation, enabling more scalable and consistent assessment. Experiments reveal significant limitations in current models, with leading systems struggling with paralinguistic control and frequent errors in situational dialogue interpretation. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.20842)

📄 **WavAlign: Enhancing Intelligence and Expressiveness in Spoken Dialogue Models via Adaptive Hybrid Post-Training** — Proposes a modality-aware post-training approach that separates semantic and acoustic optimization channels for end-to-end spoken dialogue models. Unlike direct preference optimization methods that struggle with sparse supervision over dense speech generation, WavAlign constrains preference updates to semantic content while using explicit anchoring for acoustic behavior. The method dynamically balances these components based on rollout statistics, showing improvements in both semantic quality and speech expressiveness. [Link](https://huggingface.co/papers/2604.14932)

📄 **MoVE: Translating Laughter and Tears via Mixture of Vocalization Experts in Speech-to-Speech Translation** — Addresses the loss of non-verbal vocalizations like laughter and crying in speech-to-speech translation through a Mixture-of-LoRA-Experts architecture with specialized adapters and soft routing. The approach achieves 76% reproduction of target non-verbal vocalizations compared to existing systems' 14%, demonstrating remarkable data efficiency with only 30 minutes of curated expressive data. While limited to English-Chinese translation in evaluation, the method represents a significant advance in preserving expressive elements during cross-lingual speech translation. [Link](https://huggingface.co/papers/2604.17435)
