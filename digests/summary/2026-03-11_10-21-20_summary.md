📄 **VoxEmo: Benchmarking Speech Emotion Recognition with Speech LLMs** — Introduces a comprehensive benchmark for evaluating Speech Large Language Models on emotion recognition across 35 corpora in 15 languages. Unlike existing benchmarks that use simple closed-set classification, VoxEmo employs a distribution-aware soft-label protocol and prompt-ensemble strategy to better reflect human emotion perception ambiguity. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.08936)

📄 **EmoSURA: Towards Accurate Evaluation of Detailed and Long-Context Emotional Speech Captions** — Proposes an evaluation framework that decomposes complex emotional speech descriptions into "Atomic Perceptual Units" and verifies each against the raw audio signal. This approach avoids the semantic limitations of N-gram metrics and reasoning inconsistencies of LLM judges when processing long-form descriptions. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.09820)

📄 **Universal Speech Content Factorization** — Presents a linear method that separates phonetic content from speaker timbre using least-squares optimization, requiring only seconds of target speaker data for voice conversion. Unlike previous closed-set approaches, USCF works in open-set settings with unseen speakers and serves as an efficient timbre-disentangled feature for TTS training. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.08977)

📄 **Textless and Non-Parallel Speech-to-Speech Emotion Style Transfer** — Introduces S2S-ZEST, a framework for transferring emotional style while preserving source content and speaker identity using semantic tokens, speaker representations, and emotion embeddings. The key advancement is enabling zero-shot emotion transfer without requiring text transcriptions or parallel training data with identical content spoken in different emotions. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.17655)

📄 **VSSFlow: Unifying Video-conditioned Sound and Speech Generation via Joint Learning** — Presents a unified flow-matching framework that jointly handles video-to-sound generation and visual text-to-speech synthesis using a Diffusion Transformer with disentangled condition aggregation. Unlike previous work treating these as separate tasks, VSSFlow demonstrates that joint training maintains superior performance while enabling combined sound and speech generation. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.24773)

📄 **Acoustic and Semantic Modeling of Emotion in Spoken Language** — Presents a comprehensive thesis on emotion modeling by jointly leveraging acoustic and semantic information across emotion-aware pre-training, conversational recognition, and style transfer. Key innovations include speech-driven pre-training without manual text annotation and hierarchical architectures with cross-modal attention for conversational contexts. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.09212)

📄 **Speech-Omni-Lite: Portable Speech Interfaces for Vision-Language Models** — Develops a cost-efficient method adding speech understanding and generation to pre-trained vision-language models using lightweight trainable modules while freezing the VL backbone. Achieves comparable performance to large omni-models trained on millions of hours using only thousands of hours through a data construction strategy generating training data from existing ASR speech-text pairs. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.09627)

📄 **Latent Speech-Text Transformer** — Addresses computational inefficiency in speech-text models by aggregating individual speech tokens into larger "latent speech patches" that serve as higher-level autoregressive units. This approach reduces sequence length imbalance between speech and text modalities, improving both computational efficiency and cross-modal alignment across scaling experiments from 420M to 7B parameters. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.06195)

📄 **Fish Audio S2 Technical Report** — Introduces an open-source TTS system enabling instruction-following control through natural language descriptions rather than traditional parameters. The system combines multi-speaker and multi-turn capabilities with natural language control through a multi-stage training pipeline including video/speech captioning and reward modeling. [Link](https://huggingface.co/papers/2603.08823)

🤖 **HumeAI/tada-1b** — TADA is a unified speech-language model achieving 1:1 alignment between text tokens and speech vectors for synchronized generation in a single autoregressive stream. Unlike traditional TTS models using fixed frame rates, TADA dynamically determines duration and prosody for each text token, eliminating computational overhead of fixed-frame-rate processing. [Link](https://huggingface.co/HumeAI/tada-1b)

🤖 **HumeAI/tada-3b-ml** — Multilingual version of TADA that maintains 1:1 alignment between text tokens and speech vectors for synchronized speech-text generation. The model dynamically determines speech prosody and duration per token rather than using fixed frame rates, though it requires transcripts for non-English audio prompts due to English-only ASR capabilities. [Link](https://huggingface.co/HumeAI/tada-3b-ml)

🤖 **HumeAI/tada-codec** — The underlying codec for TADA models that enables direct 1:1 correspondence between speech and text tokens in a unified stream. The innovation lies in the alignment mechanism and novel tokenizer design that the authors claim reduces computational overhead while maintaining synthesis fidelity compared to traditional approaches. [Link](https://huggingface.co/HumeAI/tada-codec)
