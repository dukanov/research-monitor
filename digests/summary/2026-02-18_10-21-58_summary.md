📄 **CLARITY: Contextual Linguistic Adaptation and Accent Retrieval for Dual-Bias Mitigation in Text-to-Speech Generation** — This framework simultaneously addresses accent bias and linguistic bias in TTS systems by combining contextual linguistic adaptation to localize input text with retrieval-augmented accent prompting for consistent speech generation. Unlike existing approaches that tackle these biases separately, CLARITY recognizes their interdependence and applies dual-signal optimization in a backbone-agnostic framework. Evaluation covers twelve English varieties, though generalization to other languages remains undemonstrated. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.11104)
