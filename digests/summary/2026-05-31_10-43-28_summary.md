🤖 **Irodori-TTS-600M-v3-VoiceDesign** — This Japanese TTS model introduces "Multi-modal Voice Design" by combining voice cloning with simultaneous style control through text captions and emoji annotations. Unlike traditional approaches that separate voice identity from speaking style, it allows users to clone a specific speaker while controlling their emotional delivery and vocal expressions through descriptive text prompts. The system uses a Rectified Flow Diffusion Transformer architecture at 600M parameters. [Link](https://huggingface.co/Aratako/Irodori-TTS-600M-v3-VoiceDesign)

🤖 **CosyVoice-300M** — This LLM-based TTS system integrates text normalization directly into the model, eliminating traditional frontend processing pipelines while supporting zero-shot voice cloning across 9 languages and 18+ Chinese dialects. The approach enables pronunciation inpainting for fine-grained control and achieves bi-directional streaming synthesis with 150ms latency. Evaluation shows competitive performance against both open-source and proprietary models in multilingual scenarios. [Link](https://huggingface.co/FunAudioLLM/CosyVoice-300M)
