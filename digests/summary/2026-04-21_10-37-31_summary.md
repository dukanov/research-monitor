📄 **LLM-Codec: Neural Audio Codec Meets Language Model Objectives** — This work addresses the fundamental mismatch between neural audio codecs designed for reconstruction and their use as tokenizers in language models by incorporating language modeling objectives directly into codec training. The approach adds future token prediction and semantic alignment between audio and text representations, achieving simultaneous optimization for both audio quality and token predictability. Results demonstrate significant improvements in speech coherence and perplexity while maintaining reconstruction fidelity. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.17852)

📄 **MoVE: Translating Laughter and Tears via Mixture of Vocalization Experts in Speech-to-Speech Translation** — MoVE tackles the limitation of existing speech-to-speech translation systems that strip away non-verbal vocalizations like laughter and crying. The approach uses a Mixture-of-LoRA-Experts architecture with specialized adapters to preserve these expressive elements, achieving 76% reproduction of target non-verbal vocalizations compared to 14% for current systems. The method requires only 30 minutes of curated data when leveraging pretrained AudioLLMs. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.17435)

📄 **MINT-Bench: A Comprehensive Multilingual Benchmark for Instruction-Following Text-to-Speech** — MINT-Bench introduces the first comprehensive multilingual benchmark for evaluating instruction-following TTS systems across 10 languages using a hierarchical taxonomy and hybrid evaluation protocol. Unlike existing evaluations with limited coverage, this benchmark provides detailed assessment of content consistency, instruction adherence, and perceptual quality. The evaluation reveals that even leading commercial systems struggle significantly with compositional and paralinguistic control tasks. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.17958)

📄 **Mechanisms of Multimodal Synchronization: Insights from Decoder-Based Video-Text-to-Speech Synthesis** — This paper provides mechanistic insights into how unified decoder-only transformers synchronize modalities with different temporal sampling rates, using video-text-to-speech synthesis as a controlled testbed. The work systematically analyzes positional encoding strategies, modality ordering trade-offs, and introduces TimeSync for phoneme-level temporal analysis. Rather than just demonstrating synthesis capabilities, it focuses on understanding the specific mechanisms enabling multimodal synchronization. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.17690)

📄 **Anonymization, Not Elimination: Utility-Preserved Speech Anonymization** — This work presents a two-stage framework that protects both linguistic content and acoustic identity while preserving data utility for downstream tasks through generative speech editing and flow-matching-based speaker anonymization. Unlike existing methods that often degrade acoustic continuity, this approach maintains usability for ASR, TTS, and emotion recognition. The evaluation includes more comprehensive privacy assessment using both acoustic and content-based speaker verification metrics. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.17000)

📄 **Prosody as Supervision: Bridging the Non-Verbal--Verbal for Multilingual Speech Emotion Recognition** — NOVA-ARC reformulates multilingual speech emotion recognition as "non-verbal-to-verbal transfer," using emotional non-verbal vocalizations as supervision rather than labeled verbal speech data. The approach leverages the hypothesis that prosodic emotion cues in non-verbal sounds transfer more effectively across languages than verbal speech patterns. The method uses hyperbolic geometry and optimal transport to align emotion prototypes between non-verbal source data and unlabeled verbal target speech. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.17647)

📄 **FreezeEmpath: Efficient Training for Empathetic Spoken Chatbots with Frozen LLMs** — FreezeEmpath develops an efficient training approach for empathetic spoken chatbots by keeping the underlying LLM frozen while training only additional components using existing speech instruction data. This method avoids the need for expensive empathetic speech instruction data and prevents catastrophic forgetting that occurs when fine-tuning LLMs with cross-modal empathetic data. The approach shows improved emotional expressiveness and better performance across empathetic dialogue and speech tasks. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.18159)

📄 **Hierarchical Codec Diffusion for Video-to-Speech Generation** — HiCoDiT leverages the multi-level structure of RVQ-based speech codecs for video-to-speech generation, where low-level tokens capture speaker semantics and high-level tokens encode prosodic details. Unlike existing methods that ignore speech hierarchy, the approach uses separate processing blocks conditioned on different facial features for speaker-aware content and prosodic modulation. The method shows improved fidelity and expressiveness while remaining limited by the inherent challenge of inferring complete acoustic information from visual cues. [Link](https://huggingface.co/papers/2604.15923)
