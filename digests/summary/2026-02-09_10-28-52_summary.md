📄 **STACodec: Semantic Token Assignment for Balancing Acoustic Fidelity and Semantic Information in Audio Codecs** — STACodec integrates semantic information from self-supervised learning models into neural audio codecs through targeted semantic token assignment at the first layer of residual vector quantization. Unlike existing hybrid codecs that use distillation and suffer reconstruction quality degradation, this approach maintains acoustic fidelity while enabling direct semantic token prediction during inference without requiring SSL-based tokenizers. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.06180)

📄 **PersonaPlex: Voice and Role Control for Full Duplex Conversational Speech Models** — PersonaPlex enables dynamic control of both conversational roles and voice characteristics in duplex speech models using hybrid prompts that combine text-based role conditioning with voice cloning from speech samples. Unlike existing duplex models limited to fixed roles and voices, this approach supports personalized, role-driven interactions by training on synthetic conversational data generated from open-source LLMs and TTS models. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.06053)

📄 **VowelPrompt: Hearing Speech Emotions from Text via Vowel-level Prosodic Augmentation** — VowelPrompt combines large language models with vowel-level prosodic features (pitch, energy, duration) converted to natural language descriptions for speech emotion recognition. Unlike existing LLM approaches that rely solely on text transcriptions, this method leverages the linguistic principle that vowels carry primary affective prosody, enabling joint reasoning over semantics and fine-grained prosodic variation through two-stage training with supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.06270)

📄 **AudioSAE: Towards Understanding of Audio-Processing Models with Sparse AutoEncoders** — This work systematically applies Sparse Autoencoders to interpret internal representations in audio processing models like Whisper and HuBERT, revealing that SAE features capture both acoustic and semantic information while remaining stable across training runs. Unlike previous interpretability work focused on text models, this study evaluates SAEs across all encoder layers of audio models and demonstrates practical applications such as reducing Whisper's false speech detections by 70%, with SAE features showing correlation with human EEG activity during speech perception. [Link](https://huggingface.co/papers/2602.05027)
