📄 **Disentangling Reasoning in Large Audio-Language Models for Ambiguous Emotion Prediction** — This work models emotion recognition as a distributional reasoning problem rather than predicting single emotion labels, using ambiguity-aware training objectives and structured chain-of-thought supervision. The approach addresses the inherently ambiguous nature of human emotions by aligning model predictions with human perceptual distributions across emotional interpretations. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.08230)

📄 **Accent Vector: Controllable Accent Manipulation for Multilingual TTS Without Accented Data** — The method enables accent control in multilingual TTS by fine-tuning on native non-English speech and computing "task vectors" that capture accent characteristics through model parameter differences. Unlike existing systems that require large accented datasets, this approach allows fine-grained accent strength control through vector scaling and interpolation operations. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.07534)

📄 **Learning-free L2-Accented Speech Generation using Phonological Rules** — This approach generates L2-accented English speech by applying explicit phonological transformation rules to phoneme sequences before inputting to multilingual TTS models. The method eliminates the need for large accented training datasets by systematically modeling phonetic differences through rule-based consonant, vowel, and syllable structure modifications. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.07550)

🤖 **Fish Audio S2 Pro** — This TTS model enables prosody and emotion control through natural language instructions embedded directly in text (e.g., "[whisper in small voice]") and uses a dual-autoregressive architecture with separate semantic and acoustic modeling components. The design allows compatibility with standard LLM serving optimizations for efficient streaming inference while supporting fine-grained control across 80+ languages. [Link](https://huggingface.co/fishaudio/s2-pro)
