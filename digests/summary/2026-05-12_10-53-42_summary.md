📄 **PoDAR: Power-Disentangled Audio Representation for Generative Modeling** — Introduces a framework that explicitly separates signal power from semantic content in audio representations using randomized power augmentation and latent consistency objectives. Unlike approaches focused on generator expressivity or reconstruction fidelity, this targets improving "latent modelability" through explicit factor disentanglement, achieving 2× faster convergence and improved speaker similarity when applied to F5-TTS. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10084)

📄 **Kinetic-Optimal Scheduling with Moment Correction for Metric-Induced Discrete Flow Matching in Zero-Shot Text-to-Speech** — Presents GibbsTTS, which improves metric-induced discrete flow matching by replacing heuristic schedulers with a kinetic-optimal scheduler that traverses probability paths at constant Fisher-Rao speed. The key advancement is making scheduling training-free and analytically derived rather than requiring hyperparameter search, while adding finite-step moment correction to reduce path-tracking errors. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.09386)

📄 **Evaluating the Expressive Appropriateness of Speech in Rich Contexts** — Introduces CEAEval, a framework for evaluating whether speech expression is contextually appropriate rather than just emotionally intense, addressing gaps in current evaluation methods. Unlike existing approaches that focus on emotional intensity, this assesses expressive appropriateness by considering discourse-level narrative context using a new Mandarin dataset and multi-component model with knowledge distillation and reinforcement learning. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.09413)

📄 **SDiaReward: Modeling and Benchmarking Spoken Dialogue Rewards with Modality and Colloquialness** — Presents an end-to-end reward model that evaluates spoken dialogue systems by directly processing multi-turn speech episodes and assessing both prosodic/emotional qualities and conversational naturalness. Unlike existing text-based evaluation methods, SDiaReward addresses the "modality gap" and "colloquialness gap" by operating on raw speech input and being specifically trained on preference pairs targeting acoustic and conversational aspects. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.14889)

🤖 **ScenemaAI/scenema-audio** — A zero-shot expressive voice cloning system built on an audio diffusion transformer that generates speech with dynamic emotional arcs, scene-aware ambient audio, and expressive acting capabilities controlled through XML prompts. Unlike standard TTS systems, it learns emotional speech patterns from real conversational data and can produce contextually appropriate expressive speech requiring only 10-20 seconds of reference audio for voice cloning. [Link](https://huggingface.co/ScenemaAI/scenema-audio)
