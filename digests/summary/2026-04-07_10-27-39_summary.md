📄 **AffectSpeech: A Large-Scale Emotional Speech Dataset with Fine-Grained Textual Descriptions for Speech Emotion Captioning and Synthesis** — This work introduces a large-scale emotional speech dataset annotated with natural language descriptions across six dimensions rather than predefined emotion categories. The approach uses human-LLM collaborative annotation to capture fine-grained emotional attributes, enabling more flexible and interpretable emotion representation in speech synthesis. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.04160)

🤖 **VoxCPM2** — VoxCPM2 is a 2B-parameter tokenizer-free diffusion autoregressive TTS model supporting 30 languages and trained on over 2 million hours of multilingual data. Unlike conventional approaches, it enables voice design from natural language descriptions without requiring reference audio, while maintaining real-time streaming capabilities. [Link](https://huggingface.co/openbmb/VoxCPM2)

🤖 **Higgs Audio V2 Generation 3B Base** — This 3.6B parameter TTS foundation model builds on Llama-3.2-3B with a novel DualFFN architecture that adds audio-specific processing while maintaining efficiency. Trained on 10 million hours of data, it demonstrates emergent capabilities including automatic prosody adaptation and simultaneous speech-music generation without post-training or fine-tuning. [Link](https://huggingface.co/bosonai/higgs-audio-v2-generation-3B-base)
