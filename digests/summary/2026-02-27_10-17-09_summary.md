📄 **TADA: A Generative Framework for Speech Modeling via Text-Acoustic Dual Alignment** — This work introduces a tokenization scheme that creates one-to-one synchronization between acoustic features and text tokens, enabling unified speech modeling within a single LLM stream. Unlike existing TTS systems that use fixed-frame-rate acoustic tokenization (creating longer, asynchronous sequences), TADA eliminates sequence length disparity between text and speech modalities. The approach incorporates "text-only guidance" that blends text-only and text-speech logits to leverage text-only LLM capabilities while reducing content hallucinations. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.23068)

📄 **LibriTTS-VI: A Public Corpus and Novel Methods for Efficient Voice Impression Control** — This paper tackles voice impression control in TTS by addressing "impression leakage" where synthesized voices are undesirably influenced by reference audio rather than target impressions (e.g., brightness, calmness). The authors propose a training strategy that separates speaker identity from impression using different utterances, and a reference-free model that generates speaker embeddings directly from target impressions. They contribute LibriTTS-VI, the first public voice impression dataset with standardized annotations, and demonstrate improved controllability with reduced mean squared error from 0.61 to 0.41. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.15626)
