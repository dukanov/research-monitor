📄 **T5Gemma-TTS Technical Report** — T5Gemma-TTS introduces an encoder-decoder codec language model architecture where cross-attention at every decoder layer maintains persistent text conditioning, avoiding the positional capacity competition between text and audio seen in decoder-only models. The system incorporates Progress-Monitoring Rotary Position Embedding (PM-RoPE) for duration control and processes text directly at subword level without phoneme conversion, though the model shows heavy dependence on PM-RoPE for successful synthesis.
