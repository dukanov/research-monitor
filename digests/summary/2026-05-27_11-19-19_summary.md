📄 **PilotTTS: A Disciplined Modular Recipe for Competitive Speech Synthesis** — Presents a lightweight autoregressive TTS system using Q-Former-based conditioning to decouple speaker identity from speaking style, trained on 200K hours of data. Unlike existing approaches, it provides a unified framework supporting multiple synthesis tasks (zero-shot cloning, emotion synthesis, dialect synthesis) while relying exclusively on open-source data processing tools and cross-sample paired training. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27258)

📄 **Can We Hear from Events? Generating Speech from Event Camera** — Introduces EventSpeech, which generates speech from neuromorphic event cameras that capture microsecond-precise illumination changes rather than traditional RGB frames. This approach addresses temporal granularity mismatches in conventional methods, where fixed camera exposure times blur high-frequency articulatory details, while event cameras naturally align with acoustic waveform dynamics. The method requires specialized neuromorphic hardware and introduces a new benchmark dataset (EVT-SPK). [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26672)

📄 **CosyEdit2: Speech-Editing-Oriented Reinforcement Learning Unlocks Better Zero-Shot TTS** — Proposes a two-stage training framework combining supervised fine-tuning with reinforcement learning (Group Relative Policy Optimization) for speech editing tasks. Unlike purely supervised approaches that rely on imperfect paired data, this method uses RL to optimize editing performance without requiring target speech during the second training stage. The authors demonstrate that improved speech editing capabilities also enhance zero-shot TTS performance. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.25930)
