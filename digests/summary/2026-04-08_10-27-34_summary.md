📄 **RLAIF-SPA: Structured AI Feedback for Semantic-Prosodic Alignment in Speech Synthesis** — This work applies reinforcement learning from AI feedback to optimize text-to-speech synthesis for emotional expressiveness and intelligibility, using ASR for semantic feedback and structured reward modeling across four evaluation dimensions (Structure, Emotion, Speed, Tone). Unlike existing approaches that require expensive human emotion annotations or rely on surrogate objectives, RLAIF-SPA directly optimizes perceptual quality through AI feedback, achieving a 26.1% reduction in word error rate and over 10% improvement in human evaluations compared to Chat-TTS.
