📄 **MiniMind-O Technical Report: An Open Small-Scale Speech-Native Omni Model** — This work presents a compact 0.1B-parameter multimodal model using a "Thinker-Talker" architecture that separates reasoning (full MiniMind backbone) from speech generation (4-layer component) with middle-layer semantic bridging. The key technical contribution is integrating voice conditioning directly into the audio-code context rather than using separate TTS modules, enabling streaming speech output alongside text and multimodal processing. While achieving moderate performance (~0.09 CER, ~0.60 voice similarity), the approach demonstrates feasibility of omni-modal capabilities at small scale. [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.03937)
