📄 **ID-LoRA: Identity-Driven Audio-Video Personalization with In-Context LoRA** — This work presents a unified approach for simultaneous personalized video and audio generation using a single diffusion model, where a reference image and audio clip control both the subject's visual appearance and voice characteristics. The method adapts the LTX-2 joint audio-video diffusion model using parameter-efficient LoRA fine-tuning, introducing negative temporal positioning and identity guidance techniques to maintain speaker identity consistency. Unlike existing approaches that process video and audio modalities separately, this joint modeling enables coherent audio-visual personalization with relatively small training datasets (~3K pairs).
