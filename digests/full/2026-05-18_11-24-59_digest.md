### [JAM-Flow: Joint Audio-Motion Synthesis with Flow Matching](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.23552)

**Relevance:** 70.0%

JAM-Flow presents a unified framework that jointly synthesizes facial motion and speech using flow matching with a Multi-Modal Diffusion Transformer architecture, integrating separate Motion-DiT and Audio-DiT modules through selective joint attention layers. Unlike existing approaches that treat talking head synthesis and text-to-speech as separate tasks, this method addresses both modalities simultaneously within a single model, supporting diverse conditioning inputs (text, reference audio, motion) through an inpainting-style training objective. The paper doesn't explicitly discuss limitations, though the complexity of joint multi-modal training may present scalability challenges.

**Key points:**

- JAM-Flow introduces a unified framework that jointly synthesizes facial motion and speech using flow matching, addressing the typically separate tasks of talking head synthesis and text-to-speech in a single coherent model
- The Multi-Modal Diffusion Transformer (MM-DiT) architecture integrates specialized Motion-DiT and Audio-DiT modules through selective joint attention layers, temporally aligned positional embeddings, and localized attention masking for effective cross-modal interaction
- Training uses an inpainting-style objective that enables flexible conditioning on various inputs (text, reference audio, reference motion), supporting diverse tasks like synchronized talking head generation and audio-driven animation
- The approach addresses a key gap in generative modeling where the intrinsic link between facial motion and speech has been largely overlooked by existing methods that treat these modalities separately
