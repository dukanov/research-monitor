### [gluschenko/omnivoice-onnx](https://huggingface.co/gluschenko/omnivoice-onnx)

**Relevance:** 75.0%

OmniVoice is a zero-shot text-to-speech model supporting over 600 languages, built on a novel diffusion language model architecture that enables both voice cloning from short reference audio and voice design through controllable speaker attributes (gender, age, pitch, etc.). It achieves notably fast inference (RTF as low as 0.025, 40x real-time) while claiming the broadest language coverage among zero-shot TTS models. This appears to be an ONNX-optimized version of the original model for deployment efficiency.

**Key points:**

- OmniVoice supports over 600 languages, representing the broadest language coverage among zero-shot text-to-speech models
- Novel diffusion language model-style architecture delivers both high-quality speech synthesis and superior inference speed
- Achieves RTF as low as 0.025 (40x faster than real-time) while maintaining state-of-the-art voice cloning quality
- Supports both zero-shot voice cloning from short reference audio and voice design via controllable speaker attributes (gender, age, pitch, dialect/accent, whisper)
- Provides fine-grained control through non-verbal symbols (e.g., [laughter]) and pronunciation correction via pinyin or phonemes
