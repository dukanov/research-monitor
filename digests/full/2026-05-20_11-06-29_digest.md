### [Cross-modal Consistency Guidance for Robust Emotion Control in Auto-Regressive TTS Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.13293)

**Relevance:** 70.0%

This paper introduces Cross-modal Consistency Guided Classifier-Free Guidance (CCG-CFG) to improve emotion control in auto-regressive TTS models when target emotions conflict with textual semantics. The approach uses dynamic scaling based on text-speech emotion inconsistency and replaces traditional dropout conditions with text emotion guidance, combined with a distillation strategy using hard-sample mining. Applied to CosyVoice2, the method achieves up to 12% absolute improvement in emotion recognition accuracy and 10% relative improvement in subjective scores compared to baselines like HierSpeech++ and Qwen3-TTS.

**Key points:**

- Introduces Cross-modal Consistency Guided Classifier-Free Guidance (CCG-CFG) method that uses dynamic scales based on inconsistency between text emotion and target speech emotion, replacing traditional dropout conditions
- Addresses the critical problem of degraded TTS quality when target emotions conflict with textual semantics through cross-modal consistency guidance
- Employs hard-sample mining strategy for distillation to improve the model's emotional alignment capabilities in challenging scenarios
- Achieves significant performance gains: 12% absolute improvement in emotion-recognition accuracy and 10% relative improvement in subjective scores on CosyVoice2
- Demonstrates that robust emotion control can be achieved while preserving speech intelligibility, naturalness, and overall quality across five emotional corpora
