### [Disentangling Reasoning in Large Audio-Language Models for Ambiguous Emotion Prediction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.08230)

**Relevance:** 70.0%

This paper addresses emotion recognition by modeling the inherently ambiguous nature of human emotions as a distributional reasoning problem using large audio-language models (LALMs), rather than predicting single emotion labels like existing approaches. The method combines an ambiguity-aware training objective that aligns with human perceptual distributions and structured chain-of-thought supervision to guide reasoning over emotional cues. The work claims to be the first systematic study of ambiguity-aware reasoning in LALMs and shows improvements across multiple training strategies on IEMOCAP and CREMA-D datasets.

**Key points:**

- Reformulates ambiguous emotion recognition as a distributional reasoning problem, addressing the limitation that most approaches predict single emotion labels when emotions are inherently ambiguous
- Presents the first systematic study of ambiguity-aware reasoning in large audio-language models (LALMs) for emotional understanding
- Introduces a two-component framework combining an ambiguity-aware objective that aligns predictions with human perceptual distributions and structured chain-of-thought supervision for reasoning over emotional cues
- Demonstrates consistent improvements across multiple training strategies (SFT, DPO, and GRPO) on standard emotion recognition datasets IEMOCAP and CREMA-D

### [Accent Vector: Controllable Accent Manipulation for Multilingual TTS Without Accented Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.07534)

**Relevance:** 70.0%

**Accent Vector** proposes a method to control accent in multilingual TTS by fine-tuning on native speech from non-English languages and computing "task vectors" that capture accent characteristics, enabling accent manipulation without requiring accented training data. Unlike existing TTS systems that primarily model American-accented English due to limited accented data, this approach allows fine-grained control over accent strength through vector scaling/interpolation and generalizes across multiple languages beyond English. The method enables both precise accent control and mixed-accent speech generation through compositional vector operations.

**Key points:**

- Introduces 'Accent Vector' - a novel controllable representation enabling accent manipulation in multilingual TTS without requiring accented training data, addressing the data scarcity problem for non-American English accents
- Uses cross-language transfer learning by fine-tuning TTS on native speech of non-English languages and computing task vectors to capture accent characteristics in English
- Achieves fine-grained control over accent strength through vector scaling and interpolation, enabling generation of mixed-accent speech rather than binary accent switching
- Demonstrates generalization beyond English to enable accent control across multiple languages, not just English variants
- Addresses critical gap where majority of English speakers are non-native (L2) yet current TTS systems primarily model American-accented English due to limited accented training data

### [Learning-free L2-Accented Speech Generation using Phonological Rules](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.07550)

**Relevance:** 70.0%

This paper presents a learning-free approach to generate L2-accented English speech by applying phonological rules to transform phoneme sequences before feeding them to a multilingual TTS model, focusing on Spanish- and Indian-accented English. Unlike existing methods that require large accented datasets, this approach uses explicit rule sets to model systematic phonetic differences (consonants, vowels, syllable structure) and provides fine-grained phoneme-level accent control. The authors note a trade-off between maintaining phoneme-level duration alignment and achieving natural accent realization in speech timing.

**Key points:**

- Learning-free approach that requires no accented training data, unlike existing TTS systems that need large-scale accented datasets
- Enables fine-grained phoneme-level accent controllability through phonological rules applied to phoneme sequences
- Develops rule sets for Spanish- and Indian-accented English by modeling systematic differences in consonants, vowels, and syllable structure from phonotactic constraints
- Identifies and analyzes trade-off between phoneme-level duration alignment and accent realization in speech timing
- Demonstrates effective accent transformation while preserving speech intelligibility and quality

## 🤖 Models

### [fishaudio/s2-pro](https://huggingface.co/fishaudio/s2-pro)

**Relevance:** 70.0%

Fish Audio S2 Pro is a text-to-speech model that enables fine-grained prosody and emotion control through natural language instructions embedded directly in text (e.g., `[whisper in small voice]`, `[excited tone]`), trained on 10M+ hours across 80+ languages. The key innovation is a dual-autoregressive architecture where a large "slow" model handles semantic content while a smaller "fast" model generates acoustic details, making it compatible with standard LLM serving optimizations for efficient streaming inference. The model is currently restricted to non-commercial use under a research license.

**Key points:**

- Dual-Autoregressive architecture combines 4B parameter 'Slow AR' for semantic prediction with 400M parameter 'Fast AR' for acoustic detail reconstruction, maintaining efficiency while preserving audio fidelity
- Fine-grained inline control supports 15,000+ unique free-form textual tags (e.g., '[whisper in small voice]', '[professional broadcast tone]') embedded directly within text rather than relying on fixed predefined control mechanisms
- Trained on 10M+ hours of audio data across 80+ languages using reinforcement learning alignment with RVQ-based audio codec (10 codebooks, ~21 Hz frame rate)
- Structural isomorphism to standard autoregressive LLMs enables inheritance of all LLM-native serving optimizations including continuous batching, paged KV cache, and RadixAttention-based prefix caching
- Production streaming performance achieves 0.195 real-time factor with ~100ms time-to-first-audio and 3,000+ acoustic tokens/s throughput on single H200 GPU
