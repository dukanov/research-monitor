### [T5Gemma-TTS Technical Report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01760)

**Relevance:** 70.0%

T5Gemma-TTS is an encoder-decoder codec language model for text-to-speech that maintains persistent text conditioning through cross-attention at every decoder layer, unlike decoder-only architectures where text competes with audio for positional capacity. The key innovations include using bidirectional text representations routed through cross-attention and introducing Progress-Monitoring Rotary Position Embedding (PM-RoPE) for improved duration control, built on a 4B parameter T5Gemma backbone trained on 170k hours of multilingual speech data. A notable limitation is that English results may be inflated since their training data (LibriHeavy) is a superset of the evaluation set (LibriSpeech), and some performance claims lack statistical significance despite numerical improvements.

**Key points:**

- Introduces encoder-decoder architecture for neural codec TTS that maintains persistent text conditioning through cross-attention at every decoder layer, addressing the limitation of decoder-only models where input text competes with growing audio sequences
- Proposes Progress-Monitoring Rotary Position Embedding (PM-RoPE) across all 26 cross-attention layers to inject normalized progress signals for improved duration control
- Achieves statistically significant speaker similarity improvements on Japanese over XTTSv2 (0.677 vs 0.622) and demonstrates cross-lingual capability with highest numerical Korean speaker similarity (0.747) despite Korean not being in training data
- Built on T5Gemma pretrained backbone (4B parameters) processing text directly at subword level without phoneme conversion, inheriting rich linguistic knowledge
- Disabling PM-RoPE at inference causes near-complete synthesis failure (CER degrades from 0.129 to 0.982), demonstrating the critical importance of this component

### [T5Gemma-TTS Technical Report](https://huggingface.co/papers/2604.01760)

**Relevance:** 70.0%

T5Gemma-TTS is an encoder-decoder neural codec language model for zero-shot voice cloning that uses cross-attention at every decoder layer to maintain persistent text conditioning, unlike decoder-only models where text competes with the growing audio sequence for positional capacity. The key innovation is Progress-Monitoring Rotary Position Embedding (PM-RoPE) for duration control, and the model processes text directly at subword level without phoneme conversion, achieving competitive speaker similarity results on multilingual data. A notable limitation is the heavy dependence on PM-RoPE - disabling it at inference causes synthesis to nearly completely fail (CER jumps from 0.129 to 0.982).

**Key points:**

- Novel encoder-decoder architecture addresses decoder-only TTS limitation where input text competes with growing audio sequence for positional capacity, using bidirectional text representations through cross-attention at every decoder layer
- Progress-Monitoring Rotary Position Embedding (PM-RoPE) injected in all 26 cross-attention layers enables duration control by providing normalized progress signals to help decoder track target speech length
- Achieves statistically significant speaker similarity improvement on Japanese over XTTSv2 (0.677 vs. 0.622) and strong zero-shot Korean performance (0.747) despite Korean not being in training data
- Disabling PM-RoPE at inference causes dramatic synthesis failure: character error rate degrades from 0.129 to 0.982 and duration accuracy drops from 79% to 46%, demonstrating critical importance of the component
- Built on 4B parameter T5Gemma backbone, processes text directly at subword level without phoneme conversion, trained on 170,000 hours of multilingual speech data
