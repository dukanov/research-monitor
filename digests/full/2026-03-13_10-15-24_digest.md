### [Causal Prosody Mediation for Text-to-Speech:Counterfactual Training of Duration, Pitch, and Energy in FastSpeech2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.11683)

**Relevance:** 75.0%

This paper introduces a causal framework for expressive text-to-speech that augments FastSpeech2 with counterfactual training objectives to disentangle emotional prosody from linguistic content. The key innovation is using two causal loss terms (Indirect Path Constraint and Counterfactual Prosody Constraint) that enforce emotion to affect speech only through prosody variables (duration, pitch, energy) while encouraging distinct prosody patterns for different emotions. A notable limitation is the assumption that emotional effects are fully captured by these three prosody features, which may not encompass all emotional expressions in speech.

**Key points:**

- Novel causal prosody mediation framework that uses structural causal modeling to disentangle emotional prosody from linguistic content in TTS, introducing Indirect Path Constraint (IPC) and Counterfactual Prosody Constraint (CPC) loss terms
- Achieves improved prosody manipulation and emotion rendering with higher MOS scores and emotion accuracy compared to baseline FastSpeech2, while maintaining better cross-speaker emotion transfer and intelligibility
- Enables controlled counterfactual prosody editing (e.g., same utterance with different emotions) through interpretable model that successfully separates prosody attribution
- Key limitation: assumes emotion effects are fully captured by only pitch, duration, and energy parameters, which may not capture the full complexity of emotional expression in speech
- Demonstrates how integrating causal learning principles into neural TTS architectures can improve controllability and expressiveness without compromising naturalness

### [Resurfacing Paralinguistic Awareness in Large Audio Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.11947)

**Relevance:** 70.0%

This paper addresses how Large Audio Language Models (LALMs) typically ignore paralinguistic cues (tone, emotion, etc.) and only respond to speech content. The authors propose a paralinguistic-enhanced fine-tuning (PE-FT) protocol that uses layer-wise analysis to identify which model layers process paralinguistic versus semantic information, then selectively fine-tunes only the paralinguistic layers while adding an auxiliary classification head. Their approach achieves better paralinguistic awareness than full model fine-tuning while being more computationally efficient.

**Key points:**

- Introduces paralinguistic-enhanced fine-tuning (PE-FT) protocol to resurface paralinguistic awareness in Large Audio Language Models, addressing the gap where current LALMs focus solely on content while neglecting paralinguistic cues that indicate user context
- Proposes five diverse layer-wise analyses to distinguish between paralinguistic processing layers and semantic understanding layers within LALMs, revealing architectural insights about how these models process different types of audio information
- PE-FT protocol includes selective-layer fine-tuning and auxiliary dual-level classification head, demonstrating that targeted fine-tuning can surpass all-layer fine-tuning performance while being more computationally efficient
- Identifies fundamental limitation in current content-centered LALM paradigm that overlooks paralinguistic cues (tone, emotion, prosody) which carry important contextual information in human speech interactions

### [ID-LoRA: Identity-Driven Audio-Video Personalization with In-Context LoRA](https://huggingface.co/papers/2603.10256)

**Relevance:** 70.0%

ID-LoRA jointly generates personalized video and audio in a single diffusion model, using text prompts, reference images, and audio clips to control both visual appearance and voice simultaneously. Unlike existing methods that treat video and audio separately, this approach enables synchronized audio-visual generation where the audio model has access to visual context, making it the first to personalize both modalities in one pass. The method introduces technical innovations including negative temporal positioning to separate reference from generation tokens and identity guidance to preserve speaker characteristics during denoising, achieving strong results with only ~3K training pairs.

**Key points:**

- First method to jointly personalize both visual appearance and voice in a single generative pass using In-Context LoRA adaptation of LTX-2 diffusion backbone
- Introduces negative temporal positions to place reference tokens in disjoint RoPE region, solving the challenge of distinguishing reference from generation tokens in shared positional-encoding space
- Proposes identity guidance - a classifier-free guidance variant that amplifies speaker-specific features by contrasting predictions with and without reference signal to prevent speaker characteristic dilution
- Achieves 73% preference for voice similarity and 24% improvement in cross-environment speaker similarity over Kling 2.6 Pro, while requiring only ~3K training pairs on single GPU
- Demonstrates that joint audio-video generation provides useful inductive bias for physically grounded sound synthesis, enabling text prompts to redirect speaking style and acoustic environment
