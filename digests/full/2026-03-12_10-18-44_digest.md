### [ID-LoRA: Identity-Driven Audio-Video Personalization with In-Context LoRA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.10256)

**Relevance:** 85.0%

ID-LoRA jointly generates personalized video and audio in a single model, using a reference image, audio clip, and text prompt to control both the subject's visual appearance and voice simultaneously. Unlike existing methods that handle video and audio separately, this approach adapts a joint audio-video diffusion model (LTX-2) with parameter-efficient LoRA, introducing techniques like negative temporal positioning and identity guidance to preserve speaker characteristics. The method shows promising results with only ~3K training pairs, though evaluation is primarily against one baseline (Kling 2.6 Pro).

**Key points:**

- First method to jointly personalize both visual appearance and voice in a single generative pass using ID-LoRA (Identity-Driven In-Context LoRA), addressing the limitation that existing methods treat video and audio separately
- Introduces negative temporal positions to solve the challenge of distinguishing reference tokens from generation tokens in shared positional-encoding space, placing reference tokens in disjoint RoPE region
- Proposes identity guidance, a classifier-free guidance variant that amplifies speaker-specific features by contrasting predictions with/without reference signal to prevent speaker characteristic dilution during denoising
- Achieves strong performance with minimal resources - outperforms Kling 2.6 Pro by 73% for voice similarity and 65% for speaking style in human preference studies, with 24% improvement in cross-environment speaker similarity using only ~3K training pairs on single GPU
- Joint generation provides useful inductive bias for physically grounded sound synthesis, enabling text prompts to redirect speaking style and acoustic environment beyond just voice cloning
