### [Covo-Audio Technical Report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.09823)

**Relevance:** 70.0%

Covo-Audio presents a 7B-parameter end-to-end Large Audio Language Model (LALM) that directly processes continuous audio inputs and generates audio outputs within a single unified architecture, achieving competitive performance across speech-text modeling, spoken dialogue, audio understanding, and full-duplex voice interaction tasks. The key differentiator is its end-to-end audio-to-audio capability combined with an "intelligence-speaker decoupling strategy" that separates dialogue reasoning from voice synthesis, enabling flexible voice customization while reducing deployment costs compared to traditional LALMs. The work appears to be a technical report rather than a peer-reviewed study, and deployment of such large models would likely require significant computational resources despite the proposed efficiency improvements.

**Key points:**

- Presents a 7B-parameter end-to-end Large Audio Language Model (LALM) that directly processes continuous audio inputs and generates audio outputs within a single unified architecture, achieving state-of-the-art performance across speech-text modeling, spoken dialogue, speech understanding, and full-duplex voice interaction
- Introduces Covo-Audio-Chat-FD with superior full-duplex interaction capabilities, demonstrating substantial improvements in both spoken dialogue and real-time conversational behaviors for practical robustness
- Proposes an intelligence-speaker decoupling strategy that separates dialogue intelligence from voice rendering to mitigate high deployment costs, enabling flexible voice customization with minimal text-to-speech data while preserving dialogue performance
- Demonstrates that 7B-scale models can effectively integrate sophisticated audio intelligence with high-level semantic reasoning, suggesting a scalable path toward more capable and versatile audio language models

### [OpenMOSS-Team/MOSS-VoiceGenerator](https://huggingface.co/OpenMOSS-Team/MOSS-VoiceGenerator)

**Relevance:** 70.0%

MOSS-VoiceGenerator is a 1.7B parameter text-to-speech model that creates custom speaker voices directly from free-form text descriptions, without requiring reference audio samples. Unlike traditional voice cloning systems that need audio examples, this model uses a unified architecture (MossTTSDelay) that jointly processes voice description prompts and target text to generate speech with specified timbres, emotions, and speaking styles. The approach enables zero-shot voice design for applications like audiobooks and games, though as part of a larger model family, it may require integration with other specialized components for complete production workflows.

**Key points:**

- MOSS-VoiceGenerator creates speaker timbres directly from free-form text descriptions without requiring reference audio, using instruction-timbre alignment to learn correspondence between textual descriptions and acoustic features
- The model employs MossTTSDelay architecture with unified modeling that concatenates voice description instructions and synthesis text as joint input, enabling simultaneous timbre design, style control, and content synthesis
- MOSS-TTS Family breaks traditional single-model TTS into five production-ready specialized models (TTS, TTSD, VoiceGenerator, SoundEffect, TTS-Realtime) that can work independently or be composed into complete pipelines
- The system supports zero-shot voice design for creating customized voices with highly expressive emotional delivery and human-like naturalness across multilingual contexts (Chinese and English)
- Unlike traditional TTS systems relying on predefined voices or reference audio, this approach addresses the challenge of finding suitable reference audio while improving integration and performance as a voice design layer for downstream TTS systems

### [OpenMOSS-Team/MOSS-TTS-Realtime](https://huggingface.co/OpenMOSS-Team/MOSS-TTS-Realtime)

**Relevance:** 70.0%

MOSS-TTS-Realtime is a streaming text-to-speech model designed for real-time conversational agents that conditions speech synthesis on both textual and acoustic context from previous dialogue turns, rather than generating each response in isolation. The key innovation is its native multi-turn context modeling, which maintains voice consistency and natural prosody across extended conversations by leveraging up to 32K tokens of dialogue history (approximately 40 minutes). The model is trained on 2.5 million hours of single-speaker data plus 1 million hours of multi-speaker conversational data, supporting over 10 languages with zero-shot voice cloning capabilities.

**Key points:**

- MOSS-TTS-Realtime is a context-aware streaming TTS model that conditions on dialogue history across both text and prior user acoustics, unlike conventional streaming TTS systems that synthesize each response in isolation
- The model supports exceptionally long context length of 32K tokens (approximately 40 minutes), enabling stable and consistent speech generation in extended conversations
- Trained on massive conversational datasets: 2.5 million hours of single-speaker speech and over 1 million hours of multi-speaker conversational data for highly natural prosody and human-like expressiveness
- Part of a comprehensive MOSS-TTS Family comprising 5 specialized production-ready models (TTS, TTSD, VoiceGenerator, SoundEffect, TTS-Realtime) that can work independently or as a complete pipeline
- Achieves high-fidelity voice cloning with multi-turn consistency while maintaining speaker identity coherence across extended dialogue sessions in real-time applications
