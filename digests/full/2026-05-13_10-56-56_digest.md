### [AffectCodec: Emotion-Preserving Neural Speech Codec for Expressive Speech Modeling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.11098)

**Relevance:** 70.0%

AffectCodec introduces a neural speech codec that explicitly preserves emotional information during speech compression, using emotion-semantic guided latent modulation and distillation techniques to retain emotional expressiveness alongside semantic content. Unlike existing codecs that primarily optimize acoustic reconstruction and often lose emotional cues during quantization, this approach integrates emotion preservation directly into the codec design. The authors demonstrate improved emotion consistency and perceptual quality across speech reconstruction, emotion recognition, and text-to-speech tasks without compromising content accuracy.

**Key points:**

- Existing neural speech codecs degrade emotional cues during quantization while primarily optimizing for acoustic reconstruction, leaving emotion expressiveness insufficiently modeled
- Novel emotion-guided neural speech codec framework combines emotion-semantic guided latent modulation, relation-preserving emotional-semantic distillation, and emotion-weighted semantic alignment
- Method explicitly preserves emotional information during compression while maintaining semantic fidelity and prosodic naturalness
- Evaluations demonstrate improved emotion consistency and perceptual quality without sacrificing content accuracy across speech reconstruction, emotion recognition, and text-to-speech generation tasks

### [Exploring Token-Space Manipulation in Latent Audio Tokenizers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.11192)

**Relevance:** 70.0%

This paper introduces LATTE (Latent Audio Tokenizer for Token-space Editing), which uses a fixed set of learnable latent tokens that aggregate global information across entire utterances, rather than the typical frame-level token sequences used in neural audio codecs. The key difference is that these latent tokens create a compact, non-temporally aligned bottleneck that enables simple token-space interventions—such as swapping token positions between utterances to modify global attributes like speaker identity and background noise. The approach maintains competitive reconstruction quality while enabling controllable audio manipulation for tasks like voice conversion and denoising without requiring task-specific supervised models.

**Key points:**

- LATTE introduces a novel audio tokenizer design that appends learnable latent tokens to audio features and retains only these tokens for quantization, creating a compact non-temporally aligned bottleneck
- Unlike frame-level token sequences in existing codecs, LATTE's latent tokens can aggregate global information across the full utterance while preserving competitive reconstruction quality at low bitrates
- Simple token-space interventions like swapping selected latent token positions between utterances can modify global attributes such as speaker identity and background noise
- The approach enables controllable audio manipulation for voice conversion and denoising tasks without requiring supervision or task-specific editing models
- The compact latent representation allows for studying and intervening on global factors of variation that are difficult to manipulate in traditional frame-level audio codecs

### [AuDirector: A Self-Reflective Closed-Loop Framework for Immersive Audio Storytelling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.11866)

**Relevance:** 70.0%

AuDirector is a multi-agent framework for generating long-form audio narratives that uses identity-aware preprocessing to match character profiles with appropriate voices, guided by utterance-level emotional instructions for expressive speech synthesis. The key innovation is a closed-loop self-correction mechanism that automatically detects and regenerates defective audio components, combined with human-guided refinement through natural language feedback for interactive script improvement. The framework addresses common issues in audio storytelling like voice-character mismatches and lack of quality control, though the abstract doesn't specify computational requirements or processing time limitations.

**Key points:**

- AuDirector introduces a self-reflective closed-loop multi-agent framework for immersive audio storytelling, addressing key limitations in existing approaches like mismatched character-voice alignment and insufficient self-correction mechanisms
- The Identity-Aware Pre-production mechanism transforms narrative texts into character profiles and utterance-level emotional instructions to retrieve suitable voice candidates and guide expressive speech synthesis for context-aligned voice adaptation
- A Collaborative Synthesis and Correction module implements a closed-loop self-correction mechanism that systematically audits and regenerates defective audio components to enhance quality
- The Human-Guided Interactive Refinement module enables user control by interpreting natural language feedback to interactively refine underlying scripts, improving human interactivity
- Experiments demonstrate superior performance compared to state-of-the-art baselines across structural coherence, emotional expressiveness, and acoustic fidelity metrics

### [Poly-SVC: Polyphony-Aware Singing Voice Conversion with Harmonic Modeling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12310)

**Relevance:** 70.0%

Poly-SVC is a singing voice conversion system that can process accompanied recordings with residual harmonies, unlike existing methods that require clean vocals and only capture lead melodies. The key innovation is using a Constant-Q Transform (CQT)-based pitch extractor to preserve both lead melody and harmonic content, combined with a random sampler and diffusion decoder using Conditional Flow Matching to generate natural-sounding polyphonic outputs. Experiments show it outperforms baselines in naturalness, timbre similarity, and harmony reconstruction on both harmony-rich and single-melody recordings.

**Key points:**

- Poly-SVC introduces the first zero-shot, cross-lingual singing voice conversion system specifically designed to handle residual harmonies in accompanied recordings, addressing a key limitation where existing methods fail to cleanly extract vocals without harmonic artifacts
- The system uses a novel three-component architecture: CQT-based pitch extractor (preserves both lead melody and residual harmony), random sampler (reduces CQT interference), and diffusion decoder with Conditional Flow Matching that fuses pitch, content, and timbre features
- Unlike existing SVC methods that rely on F0 extractors for clean vocals, Poly-SVC can process recordings where clean vocal extraction is unreliable, expanding SVC applications to more realistic scenarios with musical accompaniment
- Experimental results show Poly-SVC surpasses baseline models across three key metrics: naturalness, timbre similarity, and harmony reconstruction, demonstrating effectiveness on both harmony-rich and single-melody recordings

### [Towards Fine-Grained Multi-Dimensional Speech Understanding: Data Pipeline, Benchmark, and Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12036)

**Relevance:** 70.0%

This paper addresses the limitation that current speech LLMs struggle with fine-grained, multi-dimensional speech understanding beyond basic recognition, proposing a three-part solution: a data curation pipeline for high-quality spontaneous speech extraction, FMSU-Bench (a 14-dimension speech attribute benchmark), and FM-Speech model using decoupled attribute modeling with progressive curriculum fine-tuning. The key novelty is the systematic approach to multi-dimensional acoustic perception, moving beyond coarse-grained speech processing to handle complex features like micro-acoustic cues and paralinguistic signals. The evaluation shows current speech LLMs need significant improvement in this area, while FM-Speech substantially outperforms existing open-source models.

**Key points:**

- Current speech LLMs struggle with fine-grained, multi-dimensional perception, failing to disentangle complex features like micro-acoustic cues, acoustic scenes, and paralinguistic signals, which bottlenecks development of perceptive speech systems
- Introduced FMSU-Bench, a pioneering benchmark covering 14 speech attribute dimensions to rigorously assess fine-grained, multi-dimensional speech understanding capabilities of current models
- Developed FM-Speech model using decoupled attribute modeling and progressive curriculum fine-tuning framework, substantially elevating fine-grained, multi-dimensional acoustic perception
- Extensive evaluations reveal that current speech LLMs still require significant improvement in multi-dimensional, fine-grained understanding, while FM-Speech substantially outperforms current open-source models

### [p1atdev/Irodori-TTS-500M-v2-Character-Voice-SigLIP](https://huggingface.co/p1atdev/Irodori-TTS-500M-v2-Character-Voice-SigLIP)

**Relevance:** 70.0%

This Japanese TTS model uses character images instead of reference audio to control voice characteristics, building on a rectified flow diffusion transformer architecture. Unlike traditional voice cloning approaches that require audio samples, it extracts visual features from character images using SigLIP encoder to guide the vocal quality and speaking style of generated speech. The approach is limited to Japanese and relies on subjective visual impressions from character images to determine appropriate voice characteristics.

**Key points:**

- Novel approach replaces traditional reference audio conditioning in TTS with character image conditioning using SigLIP vision encoder to extract visual features
- Key innovation eliminates need for reference audio at inference time, instead generating speech style guided by visual impression from character images alone
- Model demonstrates feasibility of cross-modal conditioning between visual character appearance and vocal characteristics for Japanese text-to-speech
- Two architectural variants explored: SigLIP-based and wd-tagger-based image encoders for character voice conditioning
- Research extends Rectified Flow Diffusion Transformer architecture to incorporate visual conditioning signals for speaker style control

### [p1atdev/Irodori-TTS-500M-v2-Character-Voice-Tagger](https://huggingface.co/p1atdev/Irodori-TTS-500M-v2-Character-Voice-Tagger)

**Relevance:** 70.0%

This Japanese TTS model replaces traditional speaker conditioning with character image conditioning, using visual features from character images to control vocal quality and speaking style instead of reference audio clips. Unlike the base Irodori-TTS-500M-v2 which performs zero-shot voice cloning with audio references, this variant extracts features from character images via a wd-vit-tagger-v3 encoder and feeds them to the diffusion transformer for speech generation. The approach is novel but limited to Japanese and relies on subjective interpretation of how character appearance should map to vocal characteristics.

**Key points:**

- Novel approach replaces traditional reference audio conditioning with character image conditioning, using visual character features to guide speech generation in Japanese TTS
- Built on Rectified Flow Diffusion Transformer architecture that generates continuous audio latents, with character image features fed as conditioning signals instead of audio reference
- Uses SmilingWolf/wd-vit-tagger-v3 as image encoder to extract character features, with goal of generating speech whose vocal quality matches visual character impression
- Eliminates need for reference audio at inference time unlike original zero-shot voice cloning approach, making it more practical for character-based applications
- Part of comparative study with SigLIP-based variant, suggesting research into optimal image encoders for visual-to-audio style transfer
