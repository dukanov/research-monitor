### [MiniCPM-o 4.5: Towards Real-Time Full-Duplex Omni-Modal Interaction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.27393)

**Relevance:** 70.0%

MiniCPM-o 4.5 introduces a 9B parameter multimodal model that enables real-time full-duplex interaction, allowing simultaneous perception and generation across vision, audio, and speech modalities using their "Omni-Flow" framework that aligns inputs and outputs on a shared temporal axis. Unlike existing models that alternate between perception and response phases and only react to explicit requests, this approach enables proactive behaviors like issuing reminders based on continuous scene understanding while maintaining real-time performance on edge devices with under 12GB RAM. The model claims to approach Gemini 2.5 Flash performance in vision-language tasks and outperform larger omni-modal models like Qwen3-Omni-30B.

**Key points:**

- Introduces real-time full-duplex omni-modal interaction paradigm that enables simultaneous seeing, listening, and speaking, breaking away from traditional alternating perception-response phases
- Proposes Omni-Flow unified streaming framework that aligns omni-modal inputs and outputs along shared temporal axis, converting turn-based interaction into time-aligned full-duplex process
- Enables proactive AI behavior through continuous scene understanding, allowing models to issue reminders or comments without explicit user requests rather than purely reactive responses
- Achieves competitive performance with 9B parameters approaching Gemini 2.5 Flash in vision-language tasks while surpassing Qwen3-Omni-30B-A3B in omni-modal understanding
- Demonstrates edge deployment feasibility with real-time full-duplex interaction on devices with less than 12GB RAM through efficient architecture design and inference optimization
