### [UniAudio 2.0: A Unified Audio Language Model with Text-Aligned Factorized Audio Tokenization](https://huggingface.co/papers/2602.04683)

**Relevance:** 75.0%

UniAudio 2.0 introduces a novel audio tokenization approach called ReasoningCodec that factorizes audio into reasoning tokens (text-aligned, high-level representations for understanding) and reconstruction tokens (acoustic cues for high-fidelity waveform reconstruction), enabling a unified autoregressive model for both audio understanding and generation. This factorized design achieves understanding performance comparable to continuous representations while improving generation quality over prior discrete tokenizers, with the model trained on 100B text and 60B audio tokens. The approach demonstrates competitive in-domain performance and strong few-shot/zero-shot generalization across speech, sound, and music tasks.

**Key points:**

- ReasoningCodec introduces a novel factorized audio tokenization approach that separates reasoning tokens (text-aligned high-level analysis) from reconstruction tokens (acoustic cues), achieving understanding performance comparable to continuous representations while improving generation quality
- UniAudio 2.0 employs a unified autoregressive architecture for both text and audio processing, trained on 100B text tokens and 60B audio tokens, demonstrating strong few-shot and zero-shot generalization across speech, sound, and music tasks
- The model addresses two fundamental problems: designing audio tokenizers that serve both understanding and generation, and building audio foundation models with generalization capabilities analogous to large language models
- The factorized tokenization design enables hierarchical generation while maintaining high-fidelity waveform reconstruction, representing a significant advance over prior discrete tokenizers

### [TASTE: Text-Aligned Speech Tokenization and Embedding for Spoken Language Modeling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.07053)

**Relevance:** 70.0%

TASTE introduces a speech tokenization method that aligns speech tokens with corresponding text transcriptions during the tokenization stage, using attention-based aggregation and speech reconstruction objectives to enable joint speech-text modeling in spoken language models. Unlike previous approaches, TASTE is the first end-to-end method to automatically learn text-aligned speech representations through reconstruction, significantly reducing token sequence length while preserving paralinguistic information. The method shows competitive performance on established benchmarks (SALMON, StoryCloze) and outperforms other spoken language models on speech continuation tasks.

**Key points:**

- TASTE introduces the first end-to-end approach using reconstruction objective to automatically learn text-aligned speech tokenization, directly addressing the modality gap between speech and text during tokenization stage
- The method preserves essential paralinguistic information while dramatically reducing token sequence length through attention-based aggregation mechanism
- TASTE-based spoken language models significantly outperform other pre-trained SLMs on speech continuation tasks while maintaining comparable performance on SALMON and StoryCloze benchmarks
- Implementation uses straightforward joint modeling approach with Low-Rank Adaptation on pre-trained text LLMs, making it practically accessible for spoken language modeling

### [Reasoning Beyond Majority Vote: An Explainable SpeechLM Framework for Speech Emotion Recognition](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.24187)

**Relevance:** 70.0%

This paper introduces an explainable Speech Language Model framework for emotion recognition that generates both emotion predictions and natural language rationales explaining the reasoning behind each prediction, using lexical and acoustic cues from the speech. Unlike traditional approaches that rely solely on majority-voted labels for classification, this method frames emotion recognition as a generative reasoning task and incorporates rationale supervision from a teacher LLM during training, while also using annotator-aware evaluation metrics that credit matches with any human annotator. The approach maintains competitive performance on MSP-Podcast while producing human-judged plausible explanations, demonstrating a path toward interpretable emotion recognition systems.

**Key points:**

- Novel SpeechLM framework reframes Speech Emotion Recognition as a generative reasoning task, producing transcripts, emotion labels, and natural language rationales grounded in lexical and acoustic cues
- Unlike prior work focused on classification accuracy, this approach prioritizes explainability while maintaining competitive performance through rationale supervision from teacher LLMs
- Introduces annotator-aware evaluation that credits matches with any annotator label rather than just majority vote, addressing the inherent subjectivity in emotion annotation
- Demonstrates that incorporating rationale supervision offers a practical path toward interpretable SER without sacrificing predictive quality, with human evaluators finding the generated rationales plausible and well-grounded

### [Beyond Fixed Frames: Dynamic Character-Aligned Speech Tokenization](https://huggingface.co/papers/2601.23174)

**Relevance:** 70.0%

DyCAST introduces a neural audio codec that uses variable-frame-rate tokenization by aligning speech tokens with character-level linguistic units, rather than allocating tokens uniformly in time like existing fixed-frame-rate codecs. The approach incorporates soft character-level alignment during training and explicit duration modeling, enabling direct control over token durations at inference while using significantly fewer tokens than conventional methods. The system includes a retrieval-augmented decoding mechanism to maintain speech quality at low frame rates without increasing bitrate.

**Key points:**

- Introduces DyCAST, a novel variable-frame-rate speech tokenizer that breaks away from fixed frame rate limitations of existing neural audio codecs
- Enables dynamic tokenization through soft character-level alignment and explicit duration modeling, allowing direct control over token durations during decoding
- Achieves competitive speech resynthesis quality while using significantly fewer tokens than traditional fixed-frame-rate codecs
- Incorporates retrieval-augmented decoding mechanism to enhance reconstruction fidelity at low frame rates without increasing bitrate
