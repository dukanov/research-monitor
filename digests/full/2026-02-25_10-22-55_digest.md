### [MSR-Codec: A Low-Bitrate Multi-Stream Residual Codec for High-Fidelity Speech Generation with Information Disentanglement](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.13068)

**Relevance:** 70.0%

This paper introduces MSR-Codec, a low-bitrate audio codec that encodes speech into four separate streams representing semantic content, timbre, prosody, and residual information, enabling independent manipulation of these speech components. The key innovation is this multi-stream architecture that achieves information disentanglement while maintaining high-fidelity reconstruction at competitive low bitrates, and when used in a two-stage language model for TTS, it achieves state-of-the-art word error rates with less data than larger models. The disentangled representation also proves effective for voice conversion tasks where speaker characteristics and prosody can be manipulated independently.

**Key points:**

- Introduces a novel multi-stream residual codec that disentangles speech into four distinct streams (semantic, timbre, prosody, residual) enabling independent manipulation of different speech characteristics
- Achieves state-of-the-art Word Error Rate and superior speaker similarity compared to larger models despite using a lightweight design with minimal data requirements
- Demonstrates competitive high-fidelity speech reconstruction at low bitrates through multi-scale residual encoding architecture
- Shows effectiveness for voice conversion applications by enabling independent control of speaker timbre and prosody components
- Presents a two-stage language model approach for text-to-speech synthesis that leverages the codec's information disentanglement capabilities
