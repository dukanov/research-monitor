### [MOSS-Audio-Tokenizer: Scaling Audio Tokenizers for Future Audio Foundation Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.10934)

**Relevance:** 70.0%

MOSS-Audio-Tokenizer presents a 1.6 billion parameter audio tokenizer built on CAT (Causal Audio Tokenizer with Transformer), a purely Transformer-based architecture trained end-to-end on 3 million hours of diverse audio data. Unlike existing approaches that use pretrained encoders, semantic distillation, or CNN architectures with fixed inductive biases, this homogeneous Transformer design learns tokenization from scratch and demonstrates better scaling properties with consistent improvements across speech, sound, and music domains. The resulting discrete tokens enable competitive performance in downstream tasks like autoregressive TTS and ASR without requiring auxiliary components.

**Key points:**

- Introduces CAT (Causal Audio Tokenizer with Transformer), a purely Transformer-based architecture that learns discrete audio tokenization fully end-to-end without pretrained encoders or semantic distillation, addressing limitations of existing CNN-based approaches with fixed inductive biases
- MOSS-Audio-Tokenizer scales to 1.6 billion parameters trained on 3 million hours of diverse audio data, demonstrating predictable improvements with increased scale and consistently outperforming prior codecs across speech, sound, and music domains
- Enables the first purely autoregressive TTS model that surpasses prior non-autoregressive and cascaded systems, while also achieving competitive ASR performance without auxiliary encoders
- Proposes homogeneous, causal Transformer blocks as a unified and scalable interface for next-generation native audio foundation models, supporting high-fidelity reconstruction across diverse audio domains

### [RE-LLM: Refining Empathetic Speech-LLM Responses by Integrating Emotion Nuance](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.10716)

**Relevance:** 70.0%

RE-LLM is a speech-based large language model that incorporates dimensional emotion embeddings and auxiliary learning to generate more empathetic responses, processing speech input directly rather than relying solely on text. The key innovation is focusing on "emotional exploration" (deeper engagement) beyond just "emotional reflection," leveraging speech's richer emotional nuances that text cannot capture. The approach shows significant improvements in empathy metrics, with particularly notable gains in exploration scores (35-139% relative improvement) and modest improvements in speech emotion recognition accuracy (2-7%) across multiple datasets.

**Key points:**

- Novel RE-LLM approach integrates dimensional emotion embeddings and auxiliary learning into speech-LLMs to address the limitation that text-based LLMs capture limited emotion nuances
- Key insight that prior work focuses on emotional reflection while emotional exploration (crucial for deeper engagement) remains overlooked in empathetic AI systems
- Dramatic improvements in Exploration scores: 35.42%-139.28% gains compared to text-only baselines and 3.91%-22.64% compared to speech-LLM baselines across three datasets
- Dual performance enhancement: improves both empathetic response generation (14.79% better Emotional Reaction scores) and speech emotion recognition accuracy (2.3%-6.9% gains)
- Consistent cross-dataset validation showing statistically significant empathy improvements on IEMOCAP, ESD, and MSP-PODCAST datasets

### [MaskVCT: Masked Voice Codec Transformer for Zero-Shot Voice Conversion With Increased Controllability via Multiple Guidances](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.17143)

**Relevance:** 70.0%

MaskVCT is a zero-shot voice conversion model that uses multiple classifier-free guidances (CFGs) to provide independent control over speaker identity, linguistic content, and prosodic factors within a single model. Unlike previous approaches with fixed conditioning schemes, it allows users to selectively apply continuous/quantized linguistic features and optionally include pitch contour to balance intelligibility, speaker similarity, and prosody control. The model achieves superior target speaker and accent similarity compared to baselines while maintaining competitive intelligibility metrics.

**Key points:**

- MaskVCT introduces multiple classifier-free guidances (CFGs) for multi-factor controllability in zero-shot voice conversion, departing from fixed conditioning schemes used in previous models
- The model offers flexible control through optional use of continuous/quantized linguistic features and pitch contour, allowing users to balance speaker identity, linguistic content, and prosodic factors
- Achieves superior target speaker and accent similarities compared to existing baselines while maintaining competitive intelligibility (word/character error rates)
- Demonstrates that integrating diverse conditions in a single model can enhance both robustness and controllability in zero-shot voice conversion tasks

### [OpenMOSS-Team/MOSS-TTS-Local-Transformer](https://huggingface.co/OpenMOSS-Team/MOSS-TTS-Local-Transformer)

**Relevance:** 70.0%

MOSS-TTS is a family of open-source speech synthesis models designed for production applications, with the flagship model supporting zero-shot voice cloning, ultra-long synthesis (up to 1 hour), and fine-grained control at token and phoneme levels. The approach differs from existing work by offering specialized models for different tasks (dialogue, voice generation, sound effects, real-time synthesis) that can work independently or together, with strong multilingual and code-switching capabilities. The models are released in two sizes (8B and 1.7B parameters) to balance quality and computational requirements.

**Key points:**

- MOSS-TTS introduces a novel system design that breaks TTS workflows into five specialized production-ready models (TTS, TTSD, VoiceGenerator, SoundEffect, TTS-Realtime) that can work independently or be composed into complete pipelines
- Achieves state-of-the-art performance while providing fine-grained controllability including token-level duration control and phoneme-level pronunciation control with mixed input support (Chinese/English/Pinyin/IPA combinations)
- Demonstrates ultra-long speech generation capability of up to 1 hour with consistent speaker identity and delivery, addressing a key limitation in existing TTS systems for extended narration
- Supports zero-shot voice cloning with robust multilingual synthesis and natural code-switching within single utterances while maintaining speaker consistency across language transitions
- Offers two complementary architectures - MossTTSDelay (8B parameters) and MossTTSLocal (1.7B parameters) - providing options for different computational requirements and deployment scenarios
