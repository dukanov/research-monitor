### [Enhancing Conversational TTS with Cascaded Prompting and ICL-Based Online Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08709)

**Relevance:** 70.0%

This paper introduces a cascaded TTS framework that uses textual style tokens paired with high-quality audio prompts to enable single-shot adaptation to fine-grained speaking styles, treating audio prompting as In-Context Learning (ICL) that guides prosody and timbre without large-scale retraining. The key innovation is an ICL-based online reinforcement learning strategy that optimizes the autoregressive prosody model using subjective aesthetic rewards while maintaining intelligibility through CTC alignment constraints. The approach aims to overcome the data bottleneck of requiring massive annotated training data for expressive TTS control, with human evaluations showing improvements in naturalness and expressivity.

**Key points:**

- Introduces a data-efficient cascaded framework that pairs textual style tokens with human-curated audio prompts for single-shot adaptation to fine-grained speaking styles, avoiding the need for massive annotated training data
- Proposes novel ICL-based online reinforcement learning strategy that optimizes autoregressive prosody models using subjective aesthetic rewards while maintaining intelligibility through CTC alignment constraints
- Demonstrates that audio prompting can serve as In-Context Learning for TTS, enabling prosody and timbre control without massive parameter updates or large-scale retraining
- Achieves significant improvements in both naturalness and expressivity of synthesized speech according to comprehensive human perception evaluations, while mitigating hallucinations
