### [ResembleAI/Dramabox](https://huggingface.co/ResembleAI/Dramabox)

**Relevance:** 70.0%

Dramabox is an expressive text-to-speech system that uses prompt-driven control to generate speech with specific emotions, vocal effects (laughs, sighs, pauses), and speaker characteristics, built as a fine-tuned version of Lightricks' LTX-2.3 diffusion transformer model. Unlike traditional TTS systems that separate text content from prosodic control, Dramabox embeds all performance directions directly in the text prompt using a structured format (dialogue in quotes, stage directions outside), and can optionally clone voice timbre from a 10-second reference clip. The system appears optimized for dramatic/theatrical speech rather than neutral applications, and operates under the LTX-2 Community License which may limit commercial use.

**Key points:**

- Dramabox is a prompt-driven TTS where the text prompt controls all aspects simultaneously - speaker identity, emotion, delivery, laughs, sighs, breaths, and pauses - using a novel format with dialogue in quotes and stage directions outside quotes
- Built as an IC-LoRA fine-tune of LTX-2.3 3.3B Diffusion Transformer with flow matching, conditioned on Gemma 3 12B text embeddings, enabling expressive control through large language model representations
- Introduces dual guidance mechanisms: cfg_scale for text-faithfulness vs naturalness trade-off, and stg_scale for expressive emphasis without saturation through perturbed transformer block paths
- Achieves voice cloning with just 10+ seconds of reference audio, making it more accessible than methods requiring extensive training data
- Implements auto-rescaling CFG schedule that prevents audio clipping at high guidance scales while maintaining expressiveness
