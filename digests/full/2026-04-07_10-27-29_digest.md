### [AffectSpeech: A Large-Scale Emotional Speech Dataset with Fine-Grained Textual Descriptions for Speech Emotion Captioning and Synthesis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.04160)

**Relevance:** 70.0%

This paper introduces AffectSpeech, a large-scale emotional speech dataset annotated with fine-grained textual descriptions across six dimensions (sentiment, emotion captions, intensity, prosodic attributes, prominent segments, and semantic content) using a human-LLM collaborative annotation pipeline. Unlike existing approaches that rely on predefined emotion categories or simple continuous attributes, this work enables more flexible and interpretable emotion representation through natural language descriptions, with experiments showing improved performance in speech emotion captioning and synthesis tasks. The authors address the scalability-quality tradeoff by combining algorithmic pre-labeling, multi-LLM description generation, and human verification.

**Key points:**

- AffectSpeech introduces a large-scale emotional speech dataset with fine-grained textual descriptions, moving beyond limited predefined emotion categories to provide more flexible and interpretable representations of affective characteristics
- The dataset characterizes each utterance across six complementary dimensions: sentiment polarity, open-vocabulary emotion captions, intensity level, prosodic attributes, prominent segments, and semantic content, enabling multi-granular modeling of vocal expression
- A novel human-LLM collaborative annotation pipeline is employed that integrates algorithmic pre-labeling, multi-LLM description generation, and human-in-the-loop verification to balance annotation quality and scalability
- Annotations are reformulated into diverse descriptive styles to enhance linguistic diversity and reduce stylistic bias in downstream modeling
- Experimental results demonstrate that models trained on AffectSpeech consistently achieve superior performance across multiple evaluation settings for both speech emotion captioning and synthesis tasks

### [openbmb/VoxCPM2](https://huggingface.co/openbmb/VoxCPM2)

**Relevance:** 70.0%

VoxCPM2 is a 2B-parameter, tokenizer-free diffusion autoregressive text-to-speech model that supports 30 languages and generates 48kHz audio, trained on over 2 million hours of multilingual speech data. It introduces voice design capabilities (generating voices from natural language descriptions without reference audio) and controllable voice cloning with style guidance, while achieving real-time streaming performance. The model shows variability in voice design outputs across runs and performance differences across languages depending on training data availability.

**Key points:**

- VoxCPM2 introduces a tokenizer-free, diffusion autoregressive TTS architecture with 2B parameters trained on 2M+ hours of multilingual speech data, achieving real-time streaming with RTF ~0.3 on RTX 4090
- Novel voice design capability generates voices from natural language descriptions alone (gender, age, tone, emotion, pace) without requiring any reference audio
- Controllable cloning allows style guidance to modify emotion, pace, and expression while preserving speaker timbre from short reference clips
- Built-in AudioVAE V2 provides asymmetric encode/decode with super-resolution (16kHz input → 48kHz output) eliminating need for external upsamplers
- Voice Design and Style Control results show variability between runs, with performance varying across languages based on training data availability, and occasional instability with very long or highly expressive inputs

### [bosonai/higgs-audio-v2-generation-3B-base](https://huggingface.co/bosonai/higgs-audio-v2-generation-3B-base)

**Relevance:** 70.0%

Higgs Audio V2 is a 3.6B parameter text-to-speech foundation model trained on 10 million hours of audio data, built on Llama-3.2-3B with a novel "DualFFN" architecture that adds audio-specific processing capabilities while maintaining computational efficiency. The model demonstrates strong performance on TTS benchmarks (achieving 75.7% and 55.7% win rates over GPT-4o-mini-TTS on emotional and question categories) and exhibits emergent capabilities like automatic prosody adaptation, multi-speaker dialogue generation, and simultaneous speech-music generation, all without any post-training or fine-tuning. The approach combines a unified audio tokenizer that handles speech, music, and sound events at 25 fps with large-scale automated data annotation using multiple ASR and audio understanding models.

**Key points:**

- DualFFN architecture enhances LLM's ability to model acoustic tokens with minimal computational overhead, preserving 91% of original training speed while adding 2.2B parameters
- Novel unified audio tokenizer runs at just 25 frames per second while maintaining audio quality, first to train on 24 kHz data covering speech, music, and sound events in one system
- Achieves 75.7% and 55.7% win rates over GPT-4o-mini-tts on emotional and question categories despite having no post-training or fine-tuning
- Demonstrates emergent capabilities including automatic prosody adaptation during narration, zero-shot multi-speaker dialogues, melodic humming with cloned voice, and simultaneous speech and background music generation
- Trained on over 10 million hours of audio data using automated annotation pipeline with multiple ASR models and in-house audio understanding model
