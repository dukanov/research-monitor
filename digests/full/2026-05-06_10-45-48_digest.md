### [MiniMind-O Technical Report: An Open Small-Scale Speech-Native Omni Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.03937)

**Relevance:** 70.0%

MiniMind-O presents a compact 0.1B-parameter omni-modal model that processes text, speech, and images while generating both text and streaming speech output, using a "Thinker-Talker" architecture where a full MiniMind backbone handles reasoning and a separate 4-layer component generates speech. The key innovation is integrating voice conditioning directly into the audio-code context rather than using separate TTS modules, plus middle-layer semantic bridging between the reasoning and generation components. The model achieves moderate performance with ~0.09 character error rates and ~0.60 voice similarity scores, though the small scale likely limits overall capabilities compared to larger multimodal models.

**Key points:**

- MiniMind-O is an open 0.1B-scale omni model that accepts text, speech, and image inputs while producing both text and streaming speech outputs, with full code, checkpoints, and training datasets released for complete transparency
- The architecture uses a novel Thinker-Talker design where a full MiniMind backbone serves as the Thinker and an independent four-layer Talker handles speech generation through middle-layer semantic bridging
- Speaker control is integrated directly into the audio-code context using dedicated speaker tokens, right-aligned reference codec prompts, and precomputed CAM++ speaker embeddings, eliminating the need for separate TTS modules
- The paper identifies three scale-critical design choices for small omni models: middle-layer semantic bridging, multimodal sequence format, and parameter-efficient eight-codebook interface
- Both dense and MoE variants achieve comparable performance with CERs of 0.0897 and 0.0900 respectively, and voice-cloning similarities around 0.60, demonstrating effective scaling at the 0.1B parameter range
