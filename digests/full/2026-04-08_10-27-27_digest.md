### [RLAIF-SPA: Structured AI Feedback for Semantic-Prosodic Alignment in Speech Synthesis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.14628)

**Relevance:** 70.0%

**RLAIF-SPA** proposes using Reinforcement Learning from AI Feedback to improve text-to-speech synthesis by optimizing emotional expressiveness and intelligibility without human supervision, incorporating ASR for semantic feedback and structured reward modeling for prosodic-emotional consistency across four evaluation dimensions (Structure, Emotion, Speed, Tone). Unlike existing methods that rely on expensive emotion annotations or surrogate objectives, this approach uses AI feedback to directly optimize perceptual quality, achieving significant improvements including 26.1% reduction in word error rate and over 10% gains in human evaluations compared to Chat-TTS. The abstract does not identify obvious limitations of the proposed method.

**Key points:**

- Introduces RLAIF-SPA, a novel framework that uses Reinforcement Learning from AI Feedback to optimize emotional expressiveness and intelligibility in TTS without requiring costly human emotion annotations
- Combines ASR-based semantic accuracy feedback with structured reward modeling for prosodic-emotional consistency, enabling precise control over four dimensions: Structure, Emotion, Speed, and Tone
- Achieves significant performance improvements over Chat-TTS on LibriSpeech: 26.1% reduction in word error rate, 9.1% improvement in SIM-O, and over 10% gains in human subjective evaluations
- Addresses the key limitation where existing TTS systems produce semantically accurate but emotionally flat speech by directly optimizing perceptual emotional quality
- Demonstrates consistent improvements across diverse speech types including clean read speech, conversational dialogue, and emotional speech on multiple datasets (Libri-Speech, MELD, Mandarin ESD)
