### [S2S-Arena: Evaluating Paralinguistic Instruction Following in Speech-to-Speech Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.05085)

**Relevance:** 75.0%

S2S-Arena introduces a speech-native benchmark for evaluating speech-to-speech models on instruction following while preserving paralinguistic features like prosody and emotion, using a four-level complexity protocol and arena-style pairwise comparisons conducted directly in the speech domain. Unlike existing benchmarks that rely on text-based evaluation, this approach systematically assesses both semantic understanding and expressive speech qualities across 1,243 samples covering 100+ real-world tasks. Evaluation of 10 state-of-the-art systems revealed significant performance gaps between academic and industrial models, particularly under complex paralinguistic demands.

**Key points:**

- S2S-Arena introduces the first speech-native benchmark that explicitly evaluates paralinguistic instruction following (prosody, emotion, speaker traits) in speech-to-speech models, addressing a major gap in existing text-based evaluation methods
- The benchmark features a novel four-level interaction protocol that systematically increases paralinguistic complexity and an arena-style evaluation framework enabling reference-free, pairwise comparisons directly in speech modality
- Benchmarking results across 10 state-of-the-art S2S systems reveal substantial performance gaps between academic and industrial systems, particularly under complex paralinguistic demands
- The comprehensive evaluation dataset includes 1,243 speech samples spanning 100+ real-world tasks, providing actionable insights for building more natural and human-aligned speech agents

### [VITA-QinYu: Expressive Spoken Language Model for Role-Playing and Singing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06765)

**Relevance:** 70.0%

VITA-QinYu is the first end-to-end spoken language model that extends beyond natural conversation to support expressive tasks like role-playing and singing, using a hybrid speech-text paradigm with multi-codebook audio tokens to capture richer paralinguistic information. The key innovation is maintaining separate modalities while enabling expressive generation, trained on 15.8K hours of synthesized conversational, role-playing, and singing data. The model outperforms existing SLMs on both expressive tasks (7 percentage points on role-playing benchmarks, 0.13 points higher MOS for singing) and maintains state-of-the-art performance on standard conversational metrics.

**Key points:**

- First expressive end-to-end spoken language model that extends beyond natural conversation to support role-playing and singing generation
- Novel hybrid speech-text paradigm using multi-codebook audio tokens that enables richer paralinguistic representation while preserving clear modality separation
- Comprehensive data generation pipeline synthesizing 15.8K hours of natural conversation, role-playing, and singing data for training
- Superior performance: 7 percentage points improvement on role-playing benchmarks, 0.13 points higher MOS for singing, and state-of-the-art conversational accuracy with 1.38 and 4.98 percentage points improvement on C3 and URO benchmarks
- Open-source release with full-stack support for streaming and full-duplex interaction capabilities
