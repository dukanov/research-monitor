### [Step-Audio-R1.5 Technical Report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.25719)

**Relevance:** 70.0%

Step-Audio-R1.5 proposes using Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF) instead of Reinforcement Learning with Verified Rewards (RLVR) for training audio language models that perform chain-of-thought reasoning. The key insight is that RLVR creates a "verifiable reward trap" where models achieve high benchmark scores but lose conversational naturalness, prosodic quality, and emotional continuity by reducing continuous audio contexts to discrete text labels. The authors claim their RLHF approach maintains analytical reasoning capabilities while significantly improving the interactive experience and naturalness of long-turn spoken dialogues.

**Key points:**

- Identifies the 'verifiable reward trap' where RLVR optimization reduces continuous auditory contexts to discrete text labels, prioritizing isolated correctness over acoustic nuance and degrading conversational naturalness
- Introduces Step-Audio-R1.5 as a paradigm shift from Reinforcement Learning with Verified Rewards (RLVR) to Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF) for audio reasoning tasks
- Demonstrates that while RLVR achieves high scores on objective benchmarks, it systematically compromises prosodic naturalness, emotional continuity, and user immersion in real-world conversations
- Shows that the RLHF approach maintains robust analytical reasoning capabilities while profoundly transforming the interactive experience in long-turn spoken dialogues

### [Step-Audio-R1.5 Technical Report](https://huggingface.co/papers/2604.25719)

**Relevance:** 70.0%

Step-Audio-R1.5 introduces a shift from Reinforcement Learning with Verified Rewards (RLVR) to Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF) for training audio language models with chain-of-thought reasoning capabilities. The authors argue that RLVR creates a "verifiable reward trap" where models achieve high benchmark scores but lose conversational naturalness, prosodic quality, and emotional continuity by reducing continuous audio into discrete verification tasks. Their RLHF approach aims to maintain analytical reasoning performance while significantly improving the interactive experience and naturalness of long-turn spoken dialogues.

**Key points:**

- Introduces Step-Audio-R1.5 with a paradigm shift from Reinforcement Learning with Verified Rewards (RLVR) to Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF) specifically for audio reasoning models
- Identifies the 'verifiable reward trap' where RLVR achieves high benchmark scores but degrades real-world conversational quality by reducing dynamic audio interactions to mechanical answering machines
- Demonstrates that optimizing for isolated text-based verification systematically compromises prosodic naturalness, emotional continuity, and user immersion in long-turn dialogues
- Shows that Step-Audio-R1.5 maintains robust analytical reasoning capabilities while profoundly transforming the interactive experience for immersive spoken dialogue
- Challenges the prevailing paradigm of reducing continuous auditory contexts into discrete, verifiable text labels, questioning whether this fosters true audio intelligence
