### [CLARITY: Contextual Linguistic Adaptation and Accent Retrieval for Dual-Bias Mitigation in Text-to-Speech Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.11104)

**Relevance:** 70.0%

CLARITY is a framework that addresses two interdependent biases in text-to-speech systems: accent bias (defaulting to dominant phonetic patterns) and linguistic bias (misalignment with dialect-specific vocabulary/culture), using contextual linguistic adaptation to localize input text and retrieval-augmented accent prompting to ensure consistent speech prompts. The approach differs from existing work by simultaneously tackling both biases through dual-signal optimization in a backbone-agnostic framework, recognizing that authentic accent generation requires both phonetic accuracy and properly localized text. The evaluation is limited to twelve varieties of English, so generalization to other languages remains undemonstrated.

**Key points:**

- Identifies two coupled biases in TTS systems: accent bias (defaulting to dominant phonetic patterns) and linguistic bias (misalignment in dialect-specific lexical/cultural information), recognizing their interdependence
- Proposes CLARITY framework with dual-signal optimization: contextual linguistic adaptation to localize input text and retrieval-augmented accent prompting (RAAP) for accent-consistent speech prompts
- Introduces backbone-agnostic approach that can work with existing TTS architectures, making it broadly applicable across different model types
- Demonstrates that authentic accent generation requires both accent fidelity AND correctly localized text, challenging approaches that address only one aspect
- Evaluation across twelve English accent varieties shows improved accent accuracy and fairness, though specific performance metrics and potential limitations are not detailed in the abstract
