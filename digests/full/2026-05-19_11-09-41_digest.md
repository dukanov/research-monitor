### [TED-TTS: Training-Free Intra-Utterance Emotion and Duration Control for Text-to-Speech Synthesis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.03170)

**Relevance:** 75.0%

TED-TTS presents a training-free framework that enables fine-grained intra-utterance emotion and duration control for existing pretrained zero-shot TTS models, using segment-aware conditioning strategies with causal masking and monotonic alignment filtering. Unlike existing methods that only provide inter-utterance-level control, this approach allows smooth emotion transitions and duration adjustments within individual utterances without requiring additional training or proprietary datasets. The method relies on LLM-based automatic prompt construction using a 30,000-sample annotated dataset to eliminate manual prompt engineering.

**Key points:**

- Introduces TED-TTS, a training-free controllable framework that enables fine-grained intra-utterance emotion and duration control in pretrained zero-shot TTS models, addressing limitations of existing methods that only support inter-utterance-level control
- Proposes novel segment-aware emotion conditioning strategy using causal masking with monotonic stream alignment filtering to enable smooth intra-utterance emotion transitions while preserving global semantic coherence
- Develops segment-aware duration steering that combines local duration embedding steering with global EOS logit modulation for precise local duration adjustment with globally consistent termination
- Creates a 30,000-sample multi-emotion and duration-annotated text dataset to enable LLM-based automatic prompt construction, eliminating the need for manual segment-level prompt engineering
- Achieves state-of-the-art intra-utterance consistency in multi-emotion and duration control while maintaining baseline-level speech quality without requiring additional training

### [Voice ''Cloning'' is Style Transfer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16578)

**Relevance:** 70.0%

This paper demonstrates that voice cloning systems don't actually "clone" voices faithfully, but instead systematically apply style transfer that makes cloned voices sound more authoritative, warm, and customer-service-like than the original speakers. The authors found that listeners perceive cloned voices as more trustworthy and are more willing to share sensitive information with them, while the cloning process also homogenizes speaker characteristics like accent and speaking rate. The work is primarily a problem identification study that reveals behavioral and perceptual risks of current voice cloning technology, rather than proposing technical solutions.

**Key points:**

- Voice cloning models systematically perform style transfer rather than faithful voice cloning, making cloned voices sound more authoritative, warm, customer-service-like, and human-like compared to source voices
- Human annotators report greater trust in cloned voices than original source voices and show increased willingness to disclose sensitive personal information to them
- Voice cloning technology leads to homogenization of speaker characteristics, reducing variance in accent, speaking rate, and audio embedding space across different speakers
- The research reveals new behavioral risks of voice cloning technology, as the systematic style transfer effects can manipulate human perception and trust in unintended ways

### [Omni-Customizer: End-to-End MultiModal Customization for Joint Audio-Video Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.17488)

**Relevance:** 70.0%

Omni-Customizer presents an end-to-end framework for joint audio-video generation that can simultaneously preserve visual identities and vocal timbres across multiple interacting subjects. The key innovation is the Omni-Context Fusion module that enriches text prompts with multimodal identity cues, combined with a Masked TTS Cross-Attention mechanism to prevent "speech leakage" and Semantic-Anchored Multimodal RoPE for structured fusion of visual, audio, and text embeddings. The approach demonstrates state-of-the-art performance in dual-modal customized generation with strong identity preservation and audio-video synchronization, though no obvious limitations are mentioned in the abstract.

**Key points:**

- Novel end-to-end framework for simultaneous preservation of visual identities and vocal timbres across multiple interacting subjects, addressing a largely underexplored area in joint audio-video generation
- Introduces Masked TTS Cross-Attention (MTP-CA) mechanism specifically designed to prevent 'speech leakage' problem, a technical innovation for multimodal identity binding
- Proposes Semantic-Anchored Multimodal RoPE (SA-MRoPE) to anchor visual and audio reference tokens to semantic descriptions, enabling structured multimodal fusion
- Implements progressive in-pair to cross-pair curriculum learning strategy that facilitates learning of high-level and robust identity features without degrading foundational priors
- Achieves state-of-the-art performance across visual identity similarity, timbre consistency, audio-video synchronization, and overall video-audio fidelity metrics

### [SeamlessEdit: Background Noise Aware Zero-Shot Speech Editing with in-Context Enhancement](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.14066)

**Relevance:** 70.0%

SeamlessEdit is a zero-shot speech editing framework that can perform speech insertion and replacement in noisy environments, using frequency-band-aware noise suppression and in-context refinement strategies. Unlike existing speech editing methods that only work with clean speech, this approach specifically handles real-world scenarios where environmental noise overlaps with speech frequency bands. The paper claims superior performance over state-of-the-art methods in both quantitative and qualitative evaluations, though specific technical limitations are not detailed in the abstract.

**Key points:**

- Introduces SeamlessEdit, a noise-resilient zero-shot speech editing framework that addresses real-world environmental noise scenarios, unlike existing studies that only consider clean speech
- Proposes a frequency-band-aware noise suppression module and in-context refinement strategy to handle cases where voice and background noise frequency bands overlap
- Demonstrates significant performance improvements over state-of-the-art approaches in both quantitative and qualitative evaluations for noisy speech editing tasks
- Addresses a critical gap where environmental noise significantly degrades speech generation quality in existing zero-shot text-to-speech technologies

### [SemaVoice: Semantic-Aware Continuous Autoregressive Speech Synthesis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16964)

**Relevance:** 70.0%

SemaVoice addresses a key limitation in continuous autoregressive speech synthesis where models focus too heavily on low-level acoustic details rather than semantic coherence, leading to error accumulation during generation. The approach introduces a Speech Foundation Model (SFM) guided alignment mechanism that refines continuous speech representations to better capture semantic consistency and structural relationships, combined with a patch-wise diffusion head for synthesis. Results show competitive performance with 1.71% English WER on the Seed-TTS benchmark, though the abstract doesn't identify specific limitations of the approach.

**Key points:**

- SemaVoice addresses a fundamental mismatch in existing continuous autoregressive TTS systems where models focus excessively on low-level acoustic textures at the expense of high-level semantic coherence
- The framework introduces a Speech Foundation Model (SFM) guided alignment mechanism to refine continuous speech representations for better local semantic consistency and global structural relationships
- Combines semantic-aware representations with a patch-wise diffusion head within an autoregressive framework for high-fidelity zero-shot text-to-speech synthesis
- Achieves strong performance with 1.71% English WER on Seed-TTS benchmark while remaining competitive with state-of-the-art open-source systems
- SFM guided alignment shows significant improvements under varying representation granularities with fixed information-rate constraints, confirming the effectiveness of the semantic-aware approach
