## 🤖 Models

### [Buyaka/OmniVoice](https://huggingface.co/Buyaka/OmniVoice)

**Relevance:** 70.0%

OmniVoice is a zero-shot text-to-speech model that supports over 600 languages using a diffusion language model architecture, enabling voice cloning from short reference audio and voice design through speaker attributes. The key differentiator is its exceptionally broad multilingual coverage combined with fast inference speed (RTF 0.025, 40x real-time) compared to existing zero-shot TTS models. The model also provides fine-grained control through non-verbal symbols and pronunciation correction capabilities.

**Key points:**

- OmniVoice supports over 600 languages, representing the broadest language coverage among zero-shot text-to-speech models
- Uses a novel diffusion language model-style architecture that delivers both high quality and superior inference speed
- Achieves extremely fast inference with RTF as low as 0.025 (40x faster than real-time)
- Provides dual capabilities of voice cloning from short reference audio and voice design through controllable speaker attributes (gender, age, pitch, dialect, whisper)
- Offers fine-grained control through non-verbal symbols (e.g., [laughter]) and pronunciation correction via pinyin or phonemes
