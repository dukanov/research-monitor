### [Aratako/Irodori-TTS-600M-v3-VoiceDesign](https://huggingface.co/Aratako/Irodori-TTS-600M-v3-VoiceDesign)

**Relevance:** 70.0%

Irodori-TTS-600M-v3 is a Japanese text-to-speech model using a Rectified Flow Diffusion Transformer that introduces "Multi-modal Voice Design" - simultaneous control via text input, reference speech for voice cloning, and text captions for emotion/style direction. The key innovation is combining voice cloning with style control, allowing users to clone a specific speaker's voice while dictating exactly how they should speak (emotion, tone, delivery) through descriptive captions, plus emoji annotations for specific sounds like laughter or sighs. The main limitation is that it's Japanese-only, and at 600M parameters it's relatively large compared to some TTS models.

**Key points:**

- Introduces a novel Multi-modal Voice Design system that simultaneously conditions speech generation on three factors: text input, reference audio for voice cloning, and descriptive captions for emotion/style control, advancing beyond previous binary approaches
- Implements emoji-based granular control for physiological sounds (laughter, coughing, sighs) and emotional nuances, enabling fine-grained expressiveness beyond traditional TTS systems
- Utilizes Rectified Flow Diffusion Transformer (RF-DiT) architecture over continuous DACVAE latents for high-quality 48kHz Japanese speech synthesis with improved Real-Time Factor through duration prediction
- Integrates SilentCipher watermarking technology directly into generated audio outputs, addressing responsible AI usage concerns in synthetic speech generation
- Demonstrates that voice identity (via reference audio) can be preserved while completely controlling delivery style through text captions, enabling same-speaker multi-emotional synthesis

### [FunAudioLLM/CosyVoice-300M](https://huggingface.co/FunAudioLLM/CosyVoice-300M)

**Relevance:** 70.0%

CosyVoice is a text-to-speech synthesis system built on large language models that enables zero-shot multilingual voice cloning across 9 languages and 18+ Chinese dialects. The approach distinguishes itself by integrating text normalization directly into the model (eliminating traditional frontend processing), supporting pronunciation inpainting for fine-grained control, and achieving bi-directional streaming with 150ms latency while maintaining content consistency and speaker similarity. The evaluation shows competitive performance against both open-source and proprietary models, though comparisons are primarily model-based rather than extensive human studies.

**Key points:**

- Fun-CosyVoice 3.0 achieves state-of-the-art performance in content consistency, speaker similarity, and prosody naturalness using an LLM-based architecture for zero-shot multilingual speech synthesis
- The model supports pronunciation inpainting of Chinese Pinyin and English CMU phonemes, providing enhanced controllability for production use without traditional frontend modules
- Bi-streaming capability enables both text-in and audio-out streaming with latency as low as 150ms while maintaining high-quality output
- The RL-trained version (Fun-CosyVoice3-0.5B-2512_RL) achieves significant improvements with 0.81% CER for Chinese and 1.68% WER for English, outperforming most competing open-source models
- Comprehensive multilingual coverage including 9 common languages and 18+ Chinese dialects/accents with instruction support for languages, emotions, speed, and volume control
