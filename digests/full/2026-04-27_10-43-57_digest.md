### [UniSonate: A Unified Model for Speech, Music, and Sound Effect Generation with Text Instructions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.22209)

**Relevance:** 70.0%

UniSonate presents a unified flow-matching framework that generates speech, music, and sound effects from natural language instructions, using a Multimodal Diffusion Transformer with a novel dynamic token injection mechanism to handle both structured audio (speech/music) and unstructured audio (sound effects) in a single model. This differs from existing work by unifying previously separate tasks (text-to-speech, text-to-music, text-to-audio) under a standardized instruction interface, demonstrating that joint training across modalities actually improves performance through positive transfer learning. The authors report state-of-the-art results on instruction-based TTS and competitive performance on music and sound effect generation.

**Key points:**

- UniSonate introduces the first unified flow-matching framework capable of generating speech, music, and sound effects through a single natural language instruction interface, addressing the fundamental challenge of reconciling structured semantic representations with unstructured acoustic textures
- The novel dynamic token injection mechanism projects unstructured environmental sounds into a structured temporal latent space, enabling precise duration control within a phoneme-driven Multimodal Diffusion Transformer (MM-DiT)
- Achieves state-of-the-art performance with WER 1.47% for instruction-based text-to-speech and SongEval Coherence 3.18 for text-to-music generation while maintaining competitive text-to-audio fidelity
- Demonstrates positive transfer effects where joint training on diverse audio modalities significantly enhances structural coherence and prosodic expressiveness compared to single-task baselines, suggesting synergistic benefits of unified audio generation
