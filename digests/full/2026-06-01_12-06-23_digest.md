### [UNISON: A Unified Sound Generation and Editing Framework via Deep LLM Fusion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.31530)

**Relevance:** 75.0%

UNISON is a unified latent diffusion model that handles seven audio tasks (text-to-audio, text-to-speech, speaker cloning, audio editing, etc.) with a single set of weights, using layer-wise deep LLM fusion that injects hidden states from multiple frozen MLLM layers into corresponding MM-DiT blocks for improved semantic conditioning. The key innovation is the unified multi-task architecture where task identity is encoded through channel-wise masks and source audio via VAE-encoded concatenation, achieving competitive performance with task-specialist models while being ~4× smaller than comparable unified systems. The approach requires careful training stabilization through multi-task data synthesis and curriculum learning to handle the diverse task requirements effectively.

**Key points:**

- UNISON presents a unified latent diffusion framework that handles 7 different audio tasks (text-to-audio, text-to-speech, zero-shot speaker cloning, mixed speech-and-sound generation, scene-level audio editing, speech-in-scene editing, and timed temporal composition) within a single model with shared weights
- Novel layer-wise deep LLM fusion architecture that injects hidden states from uniformly sampled layers of a frozen MLLM into corresponding MM-DiT blocks via learned projections, providing superior instruction following compared to single-layer baselines
- Unified multi-task architecture encodes task identity solely through channel-wise masks and provides source audio through VAE-encoded channel concatenation, enabling seamless task switching without architectural changes
- Achieves competitive or superior performance compared to task-specialist models while being approximately 4x smaller (621M-732M parameters) than comparable unified systems
- Training stabilized through online GPU-side multi-task data synthesis pipeline with task-homogeneous batching and two-stage curriculum learning approach

### [Chatterbox-Flash: Prior-Calibrated Block Diffusion for Streaming Zero-Shot TTS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30748)

**Relevance:** 70.0%

Chatterbox-Flash converts a pretrained autoregressive TTS model into a block-diffusion decoder that generates tokens in parallel within blocks while maintaining streaming capability. The key contribution is addressing quality degradation from naively applying block diffusion to discrete speech tokens, where long-tail token distributions bias selection toward high-frequency tokens, solved through prior-calibrated scoring and adaptive early termination. The method achieves comparable quality to autoregressive baselines while enabling faster streaming inference with lower real-time factors.

**Key points:**

- Fine-tuning pretrained autoregressive TTS decoder into block-diffusion decoder enables parallel token generation within blocks while retaining streaming capability
- Long-tail token distribution in discrete speech tokens biases parallel position selection toward high-frequency tokens, degrading quality in naive block-diffusion approaches
- Prior-calibrated scoring technique subtracts block-level marginal token distribution to mitigate token bias without architectural modifications
- Early-decoding schedule adaptively terminates iteration based on calibrated confidence for improved efficiency
- Achieves comparable synthesis quality to strong baselines while supporting streaming inference with low time-to-first-packet and substantially lower real-time factor

### [UniAudio-Token: Empowering Semantic Speech Tokenizers with General Audio Perception](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.31521)

**Relevance:** 70.0%

UniAudio-Token enhances semantic speech tokenizers to handle general audio perception (beyond just speech) while preserving their linguistic capabilities through two innovations: Semantic-Acoustic Primitives that decompose audio into structured components (linguistic, vocal, and scene primitives), and Semantic-Acoustic Equilibrium that uses content-aware gating to restore fine-grained acoustic details. Unlike approaches that redesign tokenizers from scratch, this framework works within the existing semantic paradigm and maintains the compact single-codebook design while addressing the "acoustic blindness" problem of current semantic tokenizers. The evaluation shows it outperforms single-codebook baselines on both understanding and generation tasks when integrated with downstream LLMs.

**Key points:**

- UniAudio-Token addresses the 'acoustic blindness' limitation of semantic speech tokenizers by enabling general audio perception while preserving their compact single-codebook design and strong linguistic alignment
- Introduces two key innovations: Semantic-Acoustic Primitives (SAP) that decompose audio into linguistic content, vocal attributes, and auditory-scene primitives, and Semantic-Acoustic Equilibrium (SAE) with content-aware gating to restore fine-grained acoustic details
- Rather than replacing the semantic paradigm, the framework mitigates information loss through structured supervision and adaptive acoustic detail restoration from shallow layers
- Demonstrates superior performance over all single-codebook baseline tokenizers on both understanding and generation tasks when integrated with downstream LLMs, serving as a unified audio interface
- Provides comprehensive open-source release including training/inference scripts and model checkpoints, enabling broader research adoption

### [OpenSTBench: Beyond Semantic Evaluation for Speech Translation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30792)

**Relevance:** 70.0%

OpenSTBench introduces a unified evaluation framework that comprehensively assesses speech translation systems across multiple dimensions (translation quality, speech quality, temporal consistency, latency) in a single protocol, supporting both speech-to-text and speech-to-speech systems in offline and streaming settings. Unlike existing approaches that evaluate these aspects separately under different protocols, OpenSTBench organizes heterogeneous speech translation outputs into a shared evaluation format, enabling direct comparison across different system types. The framework revealed that systems with strong translation quality can still differ substantially in speech and temporal quality, highlighting the need for multidimensional evaluation.

**Key points:**

- OpenSTBench introduces a unified multidimensional evaluation framework that can assess heterogeneous speech translation systems (S2TT, S2ST, offline, streaming) under a single protocol, addressing the limitation of existing separate evaluation approaches
- The framework jointly evaluates six key dimensions: translation quality, speech quality, speaker preservation, emotion and paralinguistic fidelity, temporal consistency, and latency
- Experimental findings reveal that systems with strong translation quality can still differ substantially in speech quality and temporal quality, highlighting the importance of multidimensional evaluation
- The approach organizes heterogeneous speech translation outputs into a shared evaluation format, enabling comprehensive comparison across different system architectures and modalities

### [ImmersiveTTS: Environment-Aware Text-to-Speech with Multimodal Diffusion Transformer and Domain-Specific Representation Alignment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30965)

**Relevance:** 70.0%

ImmersiveTTS generates text-to-speech that is naturally integrated with environmental audio contexts by using a multimodal diffusion transformer that explicitly models cross-modal interactions between speech and environmental sounds through joint attention mechanisms. Unlike conventional TTS systems that generate speech in isolation, this approach considers environmental context and introduces domain-specific representation alignment using complementary self-supervised encoders to maintain semantic consistency between speech and audio domains. The work addresses the challenge of acoustic pattern and temporal dynamic disparities between speech and environmental audio, though the inherent complexity of this cross-modal generation task remains substantial.

**Key points:**

- ImmersiveTTS introduces environment-aware TTS that generates speech seamlessly integrated within environmental contexts by explicitly modeling cross-modal interactions
- Novel multimodal diffusion transformer architecture fuses transcript-aligned speech latent with text-conditioned environmental context via joint attention mechanism
- Domain-specific representation alignment objective leverages complementary self-supervised representations from speech and audio encoders to enhance semantic consistency
- Addresses the challenging problem of jointly generating speech with environmental audio, which was difficult due to disparities in acoustic patterns and temporal dynamics
- Demonstrates superior performance over existing approaches in naturalness, intelligibility, and audio fidelity across both objective metrics and human listening tests

### [SwanVoice: Expressive Long-Form Zero-Shot Speech Synthesis for Both Monologue and Dialogue](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30993)

**Relevance:** 70.0%

SwanVoice is a zero-shot text-to-speech model designed to synthesize expressive long-form speech for both monologue and multi-speaker dialogue (1-4 speakers), using a VAE-based architecture with flow-matching and speaker-turn conditioning trained progressively on monologue, mixed, and real dialogue data. Unlike existing approaches that synthesize dialogue by stitching together separately generated speaker turns (which breaks acoustic consistency and conversational coherence), SwanVoice directly generates multi-speaker dialogue while maintaining expressive coherence and controllable speaker switching. The main limitation is content accuracy, though the model achieves higher richness and hierarchy scores than open-source baselines on their evaluation benchmark.

**Key points:**

- SwanVoice introduces a novel zero-shot TTS model that handles both monologue and dialogue synthesis for 1-4 speakers, addressing the common limitation of existing systems that stitch together individual speaker outputs
- The system uses a progressive training approach: starting from monologue speech, moving through mixed and real dialogue data, then applying DiffusionNFT post-training with phone-level and speaker-similarity rewards
- SwanVoice combines a 25 Hz VAE with raw-text conditioning (including pause-aware symbols and pinyin substitution) and a flow-matching DiT with speaker-turn conditioning to maintain acoustic consistency and conversational coherence
- On SwanBench-Speech evaluation, SwanVoice achieves higher richness and hierarchy scores than all open-source baselines in both monologue and dialogue settings
- Content accuracy remains the main limitation of the system despite improvements in expressiveness and speaker consistency

### [SwanVoice: Expressive Long-Form Zero-Shot Speech Synthesis for Both Monologue and Dialogue](https://huggingface.co/papers/2605.30993)

**Relevance:** 70.0%

SwanVoice is a zero-shot text-to-speech model that synthesizes long-form dialogue with 1-4 speakers in a single pass, using a flow-matching diffusion transformer with speaker-turn conditioning and a progressive training strategy from monologue to dialogue data. Unlike existing approaches that synthesize each dialogue turn separately and concatenate outputs, SwanVoice maintains acoustic consistency and conversational coherence across speaker turns by generating the entire dialogue end-to-end. The main limitation is content accuracy, though the model achieves higher richness and hierarchy scores than open-source baselines on their evaluation benchmark.

**Key points:**

- SwanVoice introduces a unified zero-shot TTS model that handles both monologue and dialogue (1-4 speakers) without stitching separate outputs, addressing acoustic consistency and conversational coherence issues in multi-speaker synthesis
- The model uses a novel training pipeline starting from monologue speech, progressing through mixed and real dialogue data, then applying DiffusionNFT post-training with phone-level and speaker-similarity rewards
- SwanData-Speech dataset is built from in-the-wild audio using Swan Forced Aligner for pause-aware word-level alignment and RobustMegaTTS3 for pronunciation-hard cases
- SwanVoice achieves higher richness and hierarchy scores than all evaluated open-source baselines on SwanBench-Speech for both monologue and dialogue settings
- Content accuracy remains the primary limitation despite improvements in expressive coherence and speaker switching capabilities

Unlike existing benchmarks that focus on limited domains and basic quality measures, this benchmark specifically addresses long-context challenges like consistency and coherence that are critical for real-world applications. The evaluation reveals that current speech generation models still struggle significantly with highly expressive scenarios and maintaining consistency compared to human recordings.

**Key points:**

- Provides extensive coverage with 1,101 samples spanning 17 common speech scenarios, addressing the limitation of existing benchmarks that are confined to limited domains
- Develops automated evaluation protocol with seven metrics along three axes (acoustics, semantics, expressiveness) to assess critical long-text factors like consistency and coherence that existing metrics overlook
- Reveals through extensive experiments that current speech generation models still struggle significantly in highly expressive scenarios and show notable gaps in consistency and hierarchy compared to real human recordings
